## What changed in understanding

The word `suggested_repair` and a non-empty array are carrier vocabulary, not
the semantic relation needed by a completeness theorem. The missing work is
not merely more E-ROW examples: it is a canonical relation from a repairable
Line-1 rejection in a declared fragment through a single edit and its local
repair effect to an associated diagnostic suggestion. This retains the value
of existing LAB payloads without letting their shapes become implicit language
semantics.
