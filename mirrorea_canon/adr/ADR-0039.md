# ADR-0039 — I3-2 multi-process runtime acceptance and owner pause

Decision date: 2026-09-02

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-042 and close I3-2 at exact source/evidence cut
`19c5b386613d6adb1f0b934e6ced81acb327d245`.

The accepted finite profile builds/checks one ordinary source in a supervisor,
derives exact per-locus images and generated request/reply edges, starts two
actual `exec` children with disjoint locus assignments and process-local stores,
and executes remote owner serve/write plus requester-local receipt over one
private QUIC reliable bidirectional stream. Image/control, exact mTLS leaf SPKI,
reciprocal preface and sealed receiver admission are separate gates. Transport
facts remain delivery evidence and cannot mint semantic authority.

The ADR-0034 bounded program and Plan 250 remain authorized and current, but
execution is owner-paused after I3-2. No semantic milestone is active. I3-3 is
the next fixed-sequence milestone and remains inactive until explicit owner/user
resume. The pause is neither blocked nor stale, does not close the program and
does not constitute official I3 entry or exit.

## Assurance and non-effects

Accepted I3-2 evidence is `runtime-monitored`. Exact child-reported request and
reply send/receive records join source/Core/artifact/edge/carrier/request/network
references to owner serve/write and requester receipt occurrences. Positive
completion requires natural zero exits observed inside the bounded
post-preflight child lifecycle; binding, wrong-peer and lifecycle falsifiers
fail closed without converting force-killed or late-observed children into
success. Independent final review found P0=0 and P1=0.

Theory/11 is unchanged. No Lean or bounded-model result, general proof,
exactly-once, bounded synchronous preflight, host scheduling guarantee, public
wire/API/ABI/platform, production/WAN support or official lifecycle transition
is claimed. Retry/reconnect/ambiguous delivery, complete network failure and
ordering semantics, C-distributed scenarios, relation/designated pressure,
network devtools and finite I3 conformance remain ordered after explicit resume.

Reopen I3-2 only for a reproducible source-free route, owner/provenance mismatch,
transport-derived authority, direct shared store or hidden retry, redaction
loss, orphan/unbounded child lifecycle, false clean shutdown, or an I3-3 direct-
consumer counterexample showing this seam cannot be controlled deterministically.
