# ADR-0014 — Delegated L2/L3 theory research

Decision date: 2026-07-21

## Decision

The canon remains the project's only normative source and the owner remains the
decision authority for L0/L1 and all reserved boundaries. Research agents may
autonomously create, compare, formalize, and test reversible candidates in
existing LAB lanes. A candidate may change canon only through an exact,
owner-maintained row in **Delegated editable targets**, followed by the frozen
cut and independent review procedure below.

LAB contains candidates, alternatives, countermodels, formal experiments,
command evidence, review history, and forward-only supersession records. A
delegated canon update contains only a current L3 hypothesis or provisional L2
working proposition within its approved target. It never grants a file merely
because the file is labelled L2/L3, and it never turns LAB evidence into
normative authority.

## Delegated editable targets

Each row must name an existing stable canon ID plus a bounded section or claim;
file-wide, prefix, and status-label grants are invalid. A row records the exact
allowed operation, maximum effect, permitted existing LAB roots, read-only
related IDs, and the owner disposition/revision that authorized it. Only the
owner may add, widen, narrow, or remove a row through proposal -> owner
disposition -> ADR / CHANGELOG -> INDEX regeneration. A canon steward may
transcribe that decision; research agents may propose a row but cannot create
one themselves.

| Exact target | Allowed canon operation | Maximum effect | Existing LAB roots | Read-only related IDs | Owner authorization |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **No active row** | No delegated canon update | LAB research only | existing documented LAB roots | all canon IDs | none |

An unlisted target remains researchable in LAB but cannot produce a delegated
canon edit. In particular, current L1 theory text, front matter, IDs,
dependencies, settled claims, and every entry of `theory/11` are not editable
through this ADR.

## Frozen-cut review and integration

For a listed target, the author first records an immutable authority/evidence
cut: governing row and authorization, target ID, allowed operation, LAB inputs,
result class, forbidden surfaces, expected falsifier, rollback condition, and
integration steward. The allowed result classes are reproduction, literal
transcription, countermodel, conditional lemma, and an experiment in the row's
existing LAB roots.

The steward then rebases the proposed change onto its intended integration base
and freezes the base revision, cited canon blobs, proposed diff, LAB artifact
digests, and rollback diff. An independent reviewer must approve that exact
frozen cut before integration. Any later rebase, conflict resolution, wording
change, registry-row change, or change to a cited or affected canon blob
invalidates the approval and requires a new frozen cut and independent review.
The steward integrates only the exact reviewed delta.

A reproducible falsifier immediately freezes downstream reliance and new
integration in LAB. The author records and reproduces it, then the steward
freezes the proposed rollback or L2-to-L3 delta. The same independent-review
rule applies before that canon change. History is not erased: LAB records the
falsifier and supersession forward-only.

## Reserved Owner Boundaries

The following always stop for owner/canon action:

- L0/L1 direction or interpretation;
- core, authority, ownership, effect, failure, or judgment primitives;
- source, public, wire, serialization, provider, transport, artifact, or
  compatibility contracts;
- SCN expectations, conformance classifications, Gate/Phase criteria, and
  lifecycle state;
- every `theory/11` mutation, including any entry's status, identity, wording,
  Lean target, assumptions, addition, removal, rename, or discharge;
- final proof status and public completion claims; and
- new evidence lanes, helper families, schemas, CI surfaces, Make targets, or
  production implementations during the current moratorium.

## Rationale

The previous owner-only wording correctly protected the project from silent
semantic drift, proof laundering, and evidence-led local optimization. It also
made the intended research loop unable to compare and falsify working
hypotheses continuously. A file-level L2/L3 label is not a safe permission
boundary: it can contain L1 text, contracts, or ledger state.

The control is therefore an exact owner-maintained target, a frozen authority
and evidence cut, adverse evidence, independent review of the final rebased
delta, non-effects, and rollback. This permits active research while preserving
the North Star, source hierarchy, DAG discipline, explicit contracts, and the
separation between conditional Lean evidence and a completed proof.

## Consequences

- `plan/02-operating-model` and `meta/agent-instructions` define the LAB
  lifecycle and serialized integration procedure; they cannot broaden this
  ADR's editable-target table.
- `theory/11` remains the sole proof-status ledger and is wholly owner
  controlled. Lean compilation is LAB evidence only unless a separate owner
  action changes the ledger.
- Existing LAB lanes may host scoped research artifacts. The moratorium still
  forbids new lanes and helpers.
- At this decision's initial state, the editable-target table has no active
  row. Therefore no delegated canon working-state update is currently allowed;
  research may still produce a decision-ready proposed row and LAB evidence.
- `research-complete` remains LAB evidence vocabulary and has no Gate, Phase,
  SCN, OBL, conformance, implementation, or public effect.

## Rejected Alternatives

1. Keep all semantic choices owner-only: preserves safety but prevents the
   owner-directed iterative research loop in LAB.
2. Treat every L2/L3 document as agent-editable: status labels do not identify
   core, contract, ledger, or lifecycle boundaries and would allow hidden L1
   choices.
3. Put the editable-target table in LAB or a new registry file: LAB cannot
   grant authority, and another canon registry would add a second mutable
   authority surface. Keeping the table here makes owner control explicit.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider this ADR when an independent review finds repeated semantic drift,
rollback cannot contain a candidate's dependency impact, the route needs a new
lane or public contract, or the owner changes the editable-target policy. Any
change follows the ordinary proposal, owner decision, ADR, changelog, and index
procedure.
