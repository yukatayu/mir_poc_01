# ADR-0014 - Standing bounded theory research

Decision date: 2026-07-21
Amended: 2026-07-21

## 2026-08-03 bounded successor

ADR-0015 replaces the file-level reserved boundary and L3-only write route
below **only** for the owner-approved Mir Theory v0 / I1+ Milestones 0--10.
Outside that bounded program, this ADR remains the default research route.

## Decision

The canon remains the project's only normative source and the owner remains the
decision authority for L0/L1 and every reserved boundary. Research agents may
autonomously choose, compare, formalize, and test reversible candidates in
existing LAB lanes. Routine target selection is not an owner-approval gate.

The only agent-maintained canon state is a reversible L3 or L2 proposition in
`working/WRK-####`. Existing theory, specification, scenario, contract, plan,
and ledger text remain read-only to this route. A working record is normative
only about its current research boundary; it does not settle its proposition or
amend the documents it cites. LAB contains candidates, alternatives,
countermodels, formal experiments, command evidence, review history, and
forward-only supersession.

## Standing eligibility predicate

An agent may open or update an L3 `WRK-####` record without an owner-listed
target when all of the following hold:

1. The record names existing canon IDs as read-only anchors and pins its
   authority/evidence base before relying on results.
2. It pre-registers a narrow question, the status quo, at least one alternative
   or expected falsifier, non-effects, and a rollback/reopen trigger.
3. Its result class is reproduction, literal transcription, countermodel,
   conditional lemma, or an experiment in an existing documented LAB lane.
4. Any retained source or test stays inside that existing lane, is explicitly
   non-production research evidence, and introduces no new helper family,
   schema, CI/Make surface, evidence lane, or public interface.
5. It does not change or reinterpret a reserved boundary below, and a
   reasonable impact read finds no conflict with a settled invariant.

The record's pre-registration must not be rewritten to fit its outcome. A
clarification is an addendum or successor record. A working proposition may
become `L2-working` only after the steward rebases and freezes the exact base,
cited blobs, LAB artifact digests, current wording, and rollback, and an
independent reviewer approves that frozen material. The L2 record binds the
author-signed Git base, SHA-256 of the normalized record text, and exact source
digests; its admission commit is signed by a distinct reviewer key. Later
changes invalidate review.

`meta/review-keys.json` is a placeholder for the future owner-managed trust
registry. It is outside the delegated `working/` surface. Until an owner
authenticates a trust anchor through a separate canon action, the validator
deliberately rejects every L2 promotion, including records that merely add
their own keys. This leaves L3 research available without pretending that two
untrusted keys establish independent review. A later authentication provider
may enable L2 only if it preserves distinct owner-managed identity binding and
the frozen-material checks.

A reproducible falsifier stops downstream reliance immediately. The author sets
the record's `Reliance status: frozen` marker immediately; no consumer may use a
frozen record as an L2 position. Record and reproduce the falsifier in LAB,
then supersede or falsify the working record forward-only. The marker is an
emergency reliance stop, not an L0/L1 decision. A durable change does not
rewrite an L2 record back to L3: retain the frozen record and create a forward
L3 successor or escalation bundle instead.

## Reserved owner boundaries

The following always stop for owner/canon action:

- L0/L1 direction or interpretation;
- core, authority, ownership, effect, failure, or judgment primitives;
- source, public, wire, serialization, provider, transport, artifact, or
  compatibility contracts;
- SCN expectations, conformance classifications, Gate/Phase criteria, and
  lifecycle state;
- every `theory/11` mutation, including any entry's status, identity, wording,
  Lean target, assumptions, addition, removal, rename, or discharge;
- final proof status, OBL discharge, and public completion claims; and
- a new evidence lane, helper family, schema, CI surface, Make target, or
  production implementation during the current moratorium.

## Rationale

The previous exact owner-target-table mechanism preserved semantic safety but
made ordinary candidate selection a bottleneck. A file-level L2/L3 label is
still not a safe grant: it can include settled semantics, contracts, or ledger
state. The replacement therefore delegates only a separate reversible annex,
with a standing negative-list boundary, pre-registered adverse evidence,
existing-lane constraints, frozen review, and forward rollback.

This supports active theory work and implementation validation without making
experiments define the theory after the fact. It preserves the North Star,
source hierarchy, DAG discipline, explicit contracts, and the distinction
between conditional Lean evidence and a completed proof.

## Consequences

- `working/README.md` defines the record shape. It cannot broaden this ADR.
- `plan/02-operating-model` and `meta/agent-instructions` define the LAB
  lifecycle and integration procedure; they cannot broaden this ADR.
- `theory/11` remains the sole proof-status ledger. Lean compilation is LAB
  evidence only unless a separate owner action changes the ledger.
- Existing LAB lanes may host scoped research artifacts and experimental code.
  The moratorium still forbids new lanes/helpers and production implementation.
- `research-complete` remains LAB evidence vocabulary and has no Gate, Phase,
  SCN, OBL, conformance, implementation, or public effect.

## Rejected alternatives

1. Keep all semantic choices owner-only: preserves safety but prevents the
   owner-directed iterative research loop.
2. Treat every L2/L3 document as agent-editable: status labels do not identify
   core, contract, ledger, or lifecycle boundaries.
3. Keep the exact owner-maintained target table: it serializes routine target
   selection without adding protection beyond a separately bounded annex.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider this ADR when independent review finds repeated semantic drift,
rollback cannot contain a candidate's dependency impact, the route needs a new
lane or public contract, or the owner changes the reserved-boundary policy. Any
change follows the ordinary proposal, owner decision, ADR, changelog, and index
procedure.
