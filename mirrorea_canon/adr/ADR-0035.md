# ADR-0035 — Three-axis project/product responsibility map

Decision date: 2026-09-01

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-038 and `arch/06-project-product-layers`. Mir / Mirrorea is read
through three independent, many-to-many coordinates:

1. semantic strata `S0 Surface` through `S6 Host`;
2. project/product responsibility layers `PL-0` through `PL-6`; and
3. lifecycle phases theory `T0--T2` and implementation `I1--I6`.

`arch/01-strata`, `arch/06-project-product-layers`, and `plan/01-phases` are
their respective authorities. No number, maturity, owner, entry or exit may be
inferred from a same-numbered value on another axis. Legacy realization
`S0--S7` and `S7 Application` are not current semantic strata.

## Responsibility consequence

PL-0 is physical substrate; PL-1 is Mir language/semantic kernel; PL-2 is the
Mirrorea fabric; PL-3 is the future safe Browser/Host participant runtime;
PL-4 is only the Shared-Space/World-Web responsibility horizon; PL-5 contains
Domain Kits/applications; and PL-6 keeps Reversed Library as a separate upper
application/project. The two satellites remain outside the numbered stack.

Upper product needs cannot become Core primitives or lower authority. Host,
process, package, route, transport, session, certificate, renderer and provider
identity cannot mint Mir authority. PL-4 chooses no concrete addressing,
linking, discovery, publication, federation or governance.

## Program consequence

ALIGN-1 is accepted as an architecture-only milestone. It changes no production
source, runtime behavior, SCN, proof/model/OBL state, sample, public contract or
official lifecycle. ALIGN-2 becomes the sole active goal and may specify the
authorized non-freezing Browser/Host/package/View/provider responsibility and
trust contracts. I3-0 remains next/not active until ALIGN-2 closes.

Theory remains T1, broad PHASE-I1 remains unaccepted, official I2 exit remains
accepted, official I3 lifecycle remains unentered, OPEN-032 remains unresolved,
and both reliable-stream candidates remain UNSELECTED.

## Reconsideration and non-effects

Reconsider if a real consumer cannot be classified without two semantic owners
or two lifecycle readings, or if the separate map cannot prevent an upper-layer
mechanism from becoming lower meaning. Reconsideration does not authorize a
North Star or guarantee change, public freeze, concrete domain Core promotion,
or Shared-Space/Reversed Library product design.

This decision does not define detailed trust tiers, BND-007 presentation-local
computation, raw FFI/provider/resource contracts, package or storage formats,
transport, codec/wire/version, browser/renderer/sandbox, engine ABI, deployment,
production or publication. Those remain later explicit gates.
