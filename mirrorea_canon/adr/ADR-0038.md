# ADR-0038 — I3-1 private adapter and encoding acceptance

Decision date: 2026-09-02

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-041 and close I3-1. The exact accepted implementation and
evidence cut is `d75fa2e716802f75f1973b63e7db82ef2e87c673`. I3-2 is now the sole
active goal; I3-3 through NEXT-0 remain ordered and inactive. Official I3
entry and exit remain unentered.

The accepted boundary includes six closed carrier families and twelve
source/Core-derived edges, exhaustive static snapshot mapping, rejection of
`AbsoluteValueStream`, a strict private provisional marker/version/u32-frame/
JSON codec, complete-frame-only typed admission, and exact receiver-retained
snapshot checking. The selected adapter is the actual localhost QUIC reliable
bidi-stream seam in the bounded Linux x86_64 profile. TLS-over-TCP remains a
deferred replacement baseline; QUIC datagrams are excluded.

## Assurance and non-effects

Finite deterministic property/mutation evidence covers the accepted strict
codec and seam. It is not coverage-guided fuzzing, a general proof, a complete
I3 runtime, or a security/platform claim. Transport, connection, certificate
and session remain non-authoritative; observer output is reference-only and
redacted. This ADR does not add retry/reconnect semantics, live authority
admission, production deployment, public wire/API/ABI/package compatibility,
or official lifecycle acceptance.

Assurance classification is `runtime-monitored` for this finite execution,
codec and admission evidence. No new `lean-proved`, `lean-stated`, or
`model-checked-bounded` result is asserted. Coverage-guided fuzzing, general
proof, platform matrix, live authority, retry/reconnect and I3-2 runtime are
`intentionally-deferred`; theory/11 is unchanged.

I3-2 may consume this boundary for actual process deployment. Reopen only for
a reproducible carrier/codec falsifier, loss of source/Core provenance or
redaction, a selected-adapter non-conformance, or a direct I3-2 consumer
counterexample.
