# ADR-0025 — M10 I1+ finite conformance profile

## Decision

Adopt spec/11 as the selected finite M10 conformance profile for frozen
`SCN-01..10`. Each SCN has one primary ordinary source plus finite named
negative source variants; SCN-09 additionally has finite named candidate patch
sources. Every source unit is parsed and classified once through the direct
path. A negative source unit terminates at its typed diagnostic; an accepted
executable unit carries its own checked identity through direct M8
`DeferredToM9`, M9 source-bound resolution, the provisional M9-to-M8
authority-inventory bridge, deterministic execution, and observer-safe
projection. No identity is shared or inferred across variants.

Profile setup is a typed, versioned input rather than source or expected
output. A primary source may be shared across SCN correspondence rows but keeps
its exact source identity. A profile binds its version, source revision, every
source-unit hash, runtime inputs, policy stamps, predicates, and `profile_hash`. It accepts no waiver carrier;
the carrier is prohibited and must remain empty. Results must be obtained from
the direct source path, not expected JSON, reports, or fixture-name dispatch.
The same profile requires per-source-unit continuity to its terminal, negative
diagnostic/span/no-mutation checks, deterministic replay, and fresh-checkout
reproduction.

Adopt the finite release-carrier correspondence rule in spec/06 and spec/11.
For every frozen positive or negative expectation, the profile's hash-bound
correspondence declaration must name its SCN/expectation id, phase, carrier kind, exact artifact
identity, source span or typed-carrier field reference, source-derived
Core/effect/obligation reference where present, typed schedule action where
present, and evidence predicate. The verifier's corresponding outcome is only
`pass` or `fail`:
missing correspondence, `N/A`, and waiver fail closed. A rejected source keeps
its own source identity and diagnostic but has no successful Core identity.
SCN-09 base and candidate patch sources are independently parsed, classified,
and checked. Each candidate is paired during candidate check/compatibility with
a versioned, hash-bound typed patch-intent carrier; only their source/intent
identities plus candidate-check and compatibility results form the checked patch
artifact. `E-PATCH-003` self-grant and `E-PATCH-002` capability-less-write are
derived at explicit patch-intent carrier field references, not supplied by a
schedule or expected verdict. Patch submission accepts only that checked
artifact.

Profile context and schedules are exogenous typed requests/context, not an
alternate program. They may invoke a source-derived handler, submit
admission/leave/checked-patch/save/release/restore/merge/observation requests,
advance clock or lease state, and provide topology or deliberately corrupted
external objects. They may not manufacture Core, grants, verification verdicts,
epochs/incarnations/capabilities/witnesses, state/relation/fallback mutations,
patch declarations, history/projection rows, or expected results. Each runtime
row separately retains program-artifact provenance and schedule-action
provenance; a purely external control row has no fabricated Core origin.
Evidence generation is separated from expectation verification: the generator
does not read the frozen predicates, while the verifier consumes actual
evidence plus the correspondence table.

Adopt the versioned, hash-bound source-bound observation-policy carrier of
spec/11 for the non-Surface parts of SCN-05 and SCN-07. It pairs exact ordinary
source and field references with finite private-policy/redaction constraints;
it may only restrict or verify source-bound observation. SCN-05's private
cross-locus `secret_key` denial and missing required failure correspondence are
validated as `VisibilityDenied` / `E-ROW-002`; SCN-07's private-like
`inventory_note` policy conflict is validated as `E-VIS-002`. Their diagnostic
locations are typed carrier field references with related source spans retained.
This is an explicit finite release-carrier change, not a claim that M6/M7
already recognizes those checks, a metadata-only widening route, a schedule
runtime test, or a public observation grammar.

Adopt one bounded M6/M7 direct-consumer seam in spec/02--04 and spec/08.
`visible observer_safe fields (...)` is a declared, source-bound subset of the
state fields, occurring at most once after the field declarations. Omission is
private-by-default. Unknown or duplicate declared fields reject. Only writes
to listed fields produce observer-publish effect and `VisibilityDenied` failure
evidence. Metadata-only visibility is rejected because it would hide
observation policy outside the checked source identity.
Within that same seam, `Role[self] at L_actor` is the authority origin and
nested `at L_owner` is the owner evaluation/request site. Their difference is
accepted; it grants no authority. The target state's declared owner must equal
`L_owner`, same-owner RHS reads resolve at `L_owner`, and the owner action
retains `RouteUnavailable` as a generated failure.

The M9-to-M8 bridge is crate-private and provisional: it translates only
M9-issued active membership/capability/witness records losslessly into the M8
authority inventory, never publishes provider proof, never changes direct M8
`DeferredToM9`, and never auto-attaches a `ContractUpdate`.
Its sealed resolution envelope is keyed to the exact residual-bearing checked
artifact, embedded M8 identity, and complete residual row; a missing,
mismatched, or partial envelope remains deferred and cannot admit execution.

Adopt spec/11's non-public, versioned, hash-bound finite typed fallback
carrier for SCN-08. It retains exactly `live → anchor → frozen`, its
lineage/capability/lease predicates, explicit fresh reacquire, write-after-
expiry reject, and rollback non-repromotion. It supplies typed negative carrier
validation for `E-DECL-001` and `E-LIN-003` correspondence. This is a finite
release carrier rather than an M6 grammar extension or public API.

## Assurance and lifecycle

M10 preserves every existing theory/11 status and adds no general theorem or
OBL. Its release record recompiles the existing finite Lean carriers and
reports their actual Lean kernel dependencies: `propext` and `Quot.sound` are
not described as axiom-free. A passing profile is separate from an explicit
phase acceptance record; that record names the validation cut, profile hash,
independent review, non-claims, and accepting authority.

### I2 entry contract

I2 remains inactive. A later I2 entry requires a new owner-directed current
roadmap that:

1. explicitly resolves or narrows the architecture/04 carrier-freeze and
   OPEN-030 reply/receipt boundary without weakening the accepted invariants;
2. defines a bounded single-process multi-locus generated-communication
   dispatch and minimal typed devtools acceptance profile from ordinary source;
3. preserves authority origin vs evaluation site, explicit communication and
   failure, no stale resurrection, observer-safe projection, and the accepted
   M10 source/Core/trace correspondence; and
4. retains public API/ABI/wire, real socket transport, deployment, and
   distributed durability as non-effects unless separately owner-authorized.

No item in this entry contract activates I2 by itself.

## Non-effects

OBL-001--025 or OBL-027, claim C-distributed or socket behavior, freeze a
public grammar/API/ABI/wire, deploy a product, complete I2+, or turn SCN-11/12
pressure rows into the official 10/10 suite.
