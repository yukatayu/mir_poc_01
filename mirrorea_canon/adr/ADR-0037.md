# ADR-0037 — I3 private reliable-stream adapter selection

Decision date: 2026-09-02

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-040. Select Candidate B, QUIC reliable stream, as the private
adapter for the active bounded I3 program. Keep Candidate A, TLS-over-TCP framed
reliable stream, as a rejected/deferred comparison and replacement baseline.
QUIC datagrams remain excluded.

This selection follows equal actual receiver-child canaries in distinct local
OS processes. Both candidates preserved the same source/Core-bound owner-request
carrier facts and normalized nine-case outcomes. Criteria 1--7 therefore tied.
Criterion 8 implementation/library maturity had no auditable winner; candidate
size and fault/configuration surface are simplicity, not maturity. Criterion 9
had no tested winner beyond Linux x86_64 localhost. Criterion 10 future browser
relevance was therefore the first material difference and selected QUIC.
Candidate A's smaller size and faster bounded canary are lower-ranked
performance/C12 simplicity evidence and do not override that fixed-order result.

## Semantic and implementation consequence

I3-1 is the sole active goal. It must place a checked, transport-neutral,
private provisional encode/decode/admission boundary between the accepted
internal semantic carrier and the selected QUIC reliable stream. Request, serve,
result and receipt remain distinct; source/Core/artifact provenance, effect,
failure, visibility and redaction survive round trip; malformed, incomplete,
oversized, unknown-version or tampered input fails before semantic admission.
Transport, address, process, connection, session and certificate remain
non-authoritative.

The I3-0 canary is evidence for transport selection only. It is not the I3-1
adapter, actual I3-2 owner runtime, durable request cache, complete I3-3 failure
matrix, or an exactly-once mechanism. I3-1 must retain the accepted common
falsifier and carry forward the bounded hardening residuals recorded by Plan 250
and Report 2603.

## Lifecycle and OPEN-032 consequence

ALIGN-0, ALIGN-1, ALIGN-2 and I3-0 are completed. I3-1 is the sole active goal;
I3-2 is next and inactive. OPEN-032 is resolved only for this bounded program by
this decision. Historical records that list it as open retain their then-current
state; this ADR is the current disposition.

Program progress is not official lifecycle entry. Official I3 remains unentered
until the ordered I3-6 acceptance action. Theory remains T1, broad PHASE-I1
remains unaccepted, and official I2 entry then exit remains unchanged.

## Rejected alternative

Do not select TLS-over-TCP framed reliable stream for I3 now. Its bounded
semantic evidence is equal and its implementation is smaller, but the latter is
lower-ranked simplicity/performance evidence rather than an implementation/
library-maturity advantage. It cannot override QUIC's first fixed-order win at
criterion 10. Retaining the implementation and comparison evidence keeps a
conservative replacement route open.

## Reconsideration and non-effects

Reconsider before I3-1 acceptance if the selected adapter loses the equal
semantic/fault floor, cannot support lossless checked carrier mapping, requires
transport-derived authority, or exposes a security/portability defect that the
deferred baseline avoids conservatively. A public compatibility freeze,
production deployment, guarantee weakening, or third candidate remains outside
this decision.

This ADR selects no public wire, codec, version, certificate representation,
API, ABI, deployment topology, supported-platform set or production security
profile. Tested evidence is Linux x86_64 localhost only. It does not claim
mutual TLS/client authentication, live semantic authority admission, complete
delivery ambiguity, durable caching, exactly-once, browser support, I3-1
completion or official I3 entry/exit.
