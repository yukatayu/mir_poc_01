# ADR-0033 — Inactive I3 entry contract and ADR-0026 program close

Decision date: 2026-08-28

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-036 and the normative contract recorded in plan/05. The future
I3 goal is to execute accepted I2 per-locus artifacts and generated
communication across at least two operating-system processes while preserving
authority, typed failure, source/Core provenance, redaction, and Mir abstract
ordering, then close a finite C-distributed profile.

This goal is **inactive**. SYS-7 records its entry conditions only.

## Candidate disposition

Retain exactly two candidates for future owner-authorized evaluation:

```text
A: TLS-over-TCP framed reliable-stream adapter — UNSELECTED
B: QUIC reliable-stream adapter — UNSELECTED
   QUIC datagram mode — not admitted or evaluated
```

This decision selects neither candidate. It chooses no version, codec, frame
or wire schema, library, certificate representation, port, retry policy,
deployment topology, or compatibility boundary. OPEN-032 remains unresolved
until a future owner-authorized I3 program supplies comparative
C-distributed evidence and an explicit selection decision.

## Preserved semantic boundary

Transport, address, process, connection, stream, session, certificate, TLS
peer identity, QUIC connection identifier, and route metadata are not
authority. They cannot mint or transfer Core, owner state, membership,
epoch/incarnation, capability, witness, policy verdict, observation
permission, or expected result.

The I2 internal carrier and any future public wire remain separate. Future
wire/codec/version work must preserve the typed carrier meaning, provenance,
failure, occurrence, visibility, and redaction fields and receives separate
owner authorization before an irreversible public freeze.

The future adapter must expose the complete plan/05 failure matrix and refine
Mir high-level request/serve, send/receive, publish/observe, grant/revoke/use,
membership/dispatch, patch/activation, and cut/later-transition edges. Stream
order is not semantic order across streams or reconnects. Retry and duplicate
handling stay explicit; no hidden retry, exactly-once, or hidden transaction is
introduced.

## Future entry evidence

A new owner-authorized program must use frozen SCN-01, SCN-02, SCN-03, and
SCN-06 as ordinary-source C-distributed gates with positive execution,
representative network/failure falsifiers, source/Core/artifact/carrier/
network/runtime correspondence, observer-safe diagnostics, exact proof/model/
runtime classification, and independent review. Existing I2 C-static/
C-runtime evidence does not satisfy this future gate by itself.

## Lifecycle and roadmap consequence

SYS-7 is completed. The ADR-0026 Mirrorea I2 Systems Foundation SYS-0--SYS-7
bounded program is closed. Plan 249 becomes a closed execution record like
Plan 247, not a current queue. There is now no active bounded program, active
semantic milestone, current execution roadmap, or active goal.

Theory remains T1. Broad PHASE-I1 remains unaccepted. Official I2 entry and
exit remain accepted under ADR-0032. I3 remains inactive. OPEN-032 remains
unresolved. Any I3 implementation, transport selection, or new bounded program
requires fresh owner direction and a new current roadmap.

## Non-effects and reconsideration

This decision changes no production source, tests, Lean/model evidence,
OBL/THM status, Core/Surface/runtime behavior, SCN expectation, public API/ABI/
wire, deployment, or production claim. It does not select TLS/TCP or QUIC,
admit datagrams, implement network transport, claim durability/exactly-once/
general ordering/fairness/security, accept broad PHASE-I1, or activate I3.

Reconsider only under new owner direction, a reproducible counterexample to
the accepted I2-to-I3 boundary, or evidence that both retained candidates
cannot conservatively implement the plan/05 contract. Historical reports,
performance opportunity, unfinished I3+, and absence of public freeze are not
reopen triggers by themselves.
