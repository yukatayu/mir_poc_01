# ADR-0032 — SYS-6 finite I2 conformance and lifecycle acceptance

Decision date: 2026-08-28

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-035 and spec/15 as the finite source-first Mirrorea I2 Systems
Foundation conformance profile. The accepted implementation/evidence cut is
`5429712de89a7e41c46cfd7fb4a39c4a492864c4`.

The profile's producer executes the accepted checked-source, projection,
admission, dispatch, local-workflow, selected-backend, and bounded-model paths.
Its verifier reads only typed inventories and fixed predicates. Accept exactly
the 22-row inventory, actual positive/falsifier bindings, property-specific
provenance joins, deterministic content-bound identity, fail-closed typed
rejection, and observer-safe serialization defined by spec/15. M10 release
orchestration, expected JSON, paths, and fixture names are not semantic inputs.

## Assurance consequence

Twenty-one rows are `runtime-monitored`. The no-source-free-authority row uses
the accepted OBL-058 bounded model and an actual runtime control and is
`model-checked-bounded`. OBL-063 records the aggregate finite SYS-6 bridge as
`runtime-monitored`; this decision adds no Lean statement/proof or general
projection, communication-completeness, concurrency, data-race, authority,
relation, save, patch, or noninterference theorem.

The four-locus local toy remains ST for its complete workflow. OW1 evidence is
the selected exactly-one-combined-owner/source-owner source fragment. The
four-locus OW1 `BackendIneligible` residual is retained rather than hidden or
counted as whole-workflow correspondence.

## Lifecycle acceptance

Apply the following authorized lifecycle transitions in this exact order:

```text
ADR-0025 I2 entry contract satisfied
  -> official I2 entry accepted
existing I2 exit criteria satisfied by SYS-3--SYS-6 evidence
  -> official I2 exit accepted
```

I2 entry is supported by ADR-0026's owner-directed current roadmap and narrow
non-public carrier path. I2 exit is supported by actual generated per-locus
artifacts, actual process-internal endpoint dispatch, the minimal typed causal
devtools/toy workflow, deterministic replay and selected ST/OW evidence, typed
negative controls, M10 regression, exact evidence classification, and final
independent ACCEPT. No criterion is weakened and no public API/ABI/wire freeze
is added.

Broad PHASE-I1 remains unaccepted. Architecture/04 stays L2-working with
OPEN-026, OPEN-027, and the full internal carrier freeze unresolved. Theory
remains T1. These are separate exact residuals and do not invalidate the
owner-directed bounded I2 entry/exit acceptance.

The runtime report's lifecycle bits remain false and its `I2 lifecycle exit`
non-claim remains correct: a producer/verifier invocation cannot authorize or
self-apply Canon lifecycle changes. This ADR is the authorized acceptance
record that evaluates the completed evidence. The report is evidence, not the
phase authority.

SYS-6 is completed. SYS-7 is the sole active ADR-0026 milestone. I3 remains
inactive; SYS-7 may record only an inactive next-program goal and entry
contract.

## OPEN-032 consequence

OPEN-032 remains unresolved. Transport selection is not an I2 exit criterion
and is not performed by this decision. Its trigger is a future
owner-authorized I3 program/entry decision after SYS-7 has recorded the
inactive contract. Candidate transport comparison, public wire design, and
implementation remain outside this program.

## Non-effects and reconsideration

This decision does not freeze final grammar, CLI, JSON, API, ABI, artifact, or
wire; select/implement real transport; activate I3; claim public/product or
production completion; provide durable distributed persistence; accept a
browser/View/renderer product; support four-locus whole-workflow OW1; or prove
general metatheory, arbitrary scheduler/fairness/relation-DAG/data-race/
exactly-once/noninterference properties.

Reconsider for a profile that accepts a missing/extra edge, moved owner,
manual interface, direct remote store, source-free authority/state, selected
ST/OW divergence, unbound/failed evidence, wrong diagnostic, stale cut/patch
mutation, relation/fallback/designated drift, observer leak, dependency from a
lower runtime layer to the conformance aggregator, M10 regression, or a fresh
counterexample to the pre-existing I2 lifecycle criteria.
