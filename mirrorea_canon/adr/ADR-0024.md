# ADR-0024 — M9 source-bound authorization and verification

## Decision

Adopt theory/18 and spec/10 as the finite M9 seam. The outer judgment consumes
the retained `A7` identity, an embedded M8 `I8` identity, and exact
source-bound `AuthDeferred(MembershipAuth)` and
`VerifyDeferred(finite_refinement)` rows. Only their combined M9 resolution
can return an M9-admitted runtime; M8 remains `DeferredToM9` by itself.

`ExtensionFrame` is shared provenance, not a common result type. Runtime
policy modules transform Contracts. The selected `MembershipAuth` and
`CapabilityAuth` profile is non-transparent, therefore uses
`ContractUpdate(old_ref, new_ref, reason, activation_cut, admitted_by,
observation_delta)`, live admission/grant lineage, and activation at the cut.
Verifier modules map a Judgment or ResidualObligation only to Evidence,
Diagnostic, or ResidualObligation. Evidence is not authority and cannot
activate a ContractUpdate.

Revocation/removal appends invalidation while retaining historical provenance;
later activation/use validates current membership/capability lineage and fails
closed. A crate-private helper may derive the embedded M8 instance only
losslessly from the retained checked artifact/canonical M8 route; it may not
reparse, rebuild Core/source maps, erase residuals, or define a public API.

The invalidation rule is a selected seam requirement. Its accepted OBL-028
evidence is limited to a fixed one-subject/one-capability reachable-state graph
to bound 4 over `admit`, `grant`, `revoke`, `use`, and `reacquire`.
Input-sensitive revocation/use/rejected-use/reacquire constraints check
monotone revocation and rejected-use-no-M8-mutation, with concrete fault
counterexamples. The Lean fixture remains its separate fixed two-frame trace.
None of this is a general proof, action-sequence enumeration theorem, or
authorization-composition theorem.

## Non-effects

This decision does not settle M9 generally, alter M7 static admissibility,
prove THM-004/005, discharge general OBL-026/028, claim M10/SCN conformance,
or define transport, public API/ABI/wire, or final grammar.
