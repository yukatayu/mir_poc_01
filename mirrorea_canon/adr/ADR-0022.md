# ADR-0022 — M7 checked elaboration over the M6 fixed input

## Decision

Adopt theory/16 and spec/08 as the M7 finite source-first checker/elaborator
boundary. `check_and_elaborate_surface_v0` takes ordinary source through the
accepted M6 broad-token parser and classifier, moves the complete accepted
`SurfaceV0Classification` into the checked artifact (published as
`consumed_m6_classification()`), then performs its finite checks and returns
either a checked artifact or `SurfaceV0PipelineDiagnostics`. It preserves the
M6 root/source `SourceRef`s, templates, and source-to-Core map rather than a
classification summary or a reconstructed map. M7 refines M6; it does not
reinterpret an M6 source form, narrow M6 expression acceptance, or install
another grammar/classifier. In particular, M6 accepts every canonical
`M6ExprToken` (`Ident`, integer, `{ } [ ] ( ) : , . = + -`) with spans; M7
alone rejects an accepted expression outside its finite tree as
`UnsupportedExpression`.

M6 diagnostics are forwarded under `M7DiagnosticKind` with their original
span. M7 adds typed finite diagnostics for duplicate declarations, unknown
state fields, a generated failure missing from `fails(...)`, and the finite
`TypeMismatch`, `UndefinedStateIndexType`, `UndefinedStateFieldType`,
`UndefinedRelationSubjectType`, `UndefinedOwnerLocus`,
`UndefinedConsumerLocus`, `UndefinedSelfPrincipal`,
`UndefinedRoleEvaluationLocus`, `DuplicateStateField`, `DuplicateEvent`,
`DuplicateRelation`, `DuplicateDesignated`, and `DuplicateDeferred` cases.
A finite expression outside the M7 ordered-tree subset is
`UnsupportedExpression`; `+`/`-` with a non-`Int` operand is
`ArithmeticRequiresInt` at the operator span. A duplicate points to its
second/offending span. Every such failure has no
executable Core.

The checked artifact retains source file/span and a total, stable
source-to-Core mapping. It records the orthogonal M3 `SemanticForm`,
`EvaluationSite`, `TriggerClock`, `AuthorityOrigin`, and `Materialization`
axes, M8-consumable Core projections, typed effect rows, and explicit
failure-row, capability, witness, authority, and evaluation obligations.
Owner RMW retains its typed, ordered, source-spanned expression tree, target,
same-owner reads, and request-to-owner/local-read/owner-write effects. Every
`EffectEntry` and `GeneratedObligation` is publicly enumerable and retains a
source span/`SourceRef`. Relation publication retains
owner/subject/type/binding-frontier/primary-fallback transform data plus its
relation-publication effect. Designated value publication retains its
logical-tick/input-frontier/result-frontier/version/bounded expression/value
effect and an
M7-specific typed request-plus-receipt-use input dependency. That dependency
is not an M5 request edge and does not define transport or delivery. It has M3
`SemanticForm::Value`, a deterministic `EvaluationPolicy`, conservative
`ObservationPolicy`, and immutable `PolicyStamp`. These
kinds remain distinct and preserve the M6/M5 frontier separation.

`Visibility`, `RelationLifetime`, `FallbackValidity`,
`ValueVisibilityRedaction`, `AuthDeferred`, and `VerifyDeferred` are residual
obligation kinds. A residual is a successful *static* elaboration
fact, never a runtime success: it grants no authority/capability, creates no
effect/mutation/verdict, and leaves `execution_is_admissible = false`.
`Authority` and `AdmittedEvaluatorAuthority` are obligations only and never
authority-success evidence.
`execution_is_admissible` is true only for a checked artifact with a nonempty
checked evaluation and an empty residual row. `ResidualCannotExecute` is the explicit result of demanding execution
admission from an unresolved residual artifact.

## Consequences

- M8 has one checked, span-preserving static artifact to consume without
  treating M6 templates or residuals as runtime authority.
- OBL-049 records only the exact finite M7 Lean evidence aligned to the M7
  source matrix and internal API names.
- M6 remains the grammar/classification owner. Its no-hidden-receipt,
  literal-self, target-owner, frontier, and deferred-template boundaries are
  preserved rather than duplicated.

## Non-effects

This decision does not prove general checker decidability or elaboration
determinism; define M8 runtime admission/scheduling; implement transport or
receipts; settle M9 membership/authentication/verification; freeze a public
diagnostic catalog or JSON/API/ABI/wire; claim SCN/C-static conformance; or
select final grammar/deployment behavior.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider if the M7 finite checker must change M6 semantics, cannot retain a
source span/map, accepts rejected source with executable Core, turns a
residual into hidden success, or requires a new syntax form rather than an
explicit later extension.
