# ADR-0018 — Evaluation / materialization calculus

## Decision

Under ADR-0015, adopt `theory/13-evaluation-materialization` as the M3
calculus. Every elaborated operation has an `EvalPlan` with five independent
coordinates:

```text
semantic form       value | state | relation | computation
evaluation site     owner(O) | locus(L) | designated(E) | consumer(C) | provider(P)
trigger / clock     on-request | on-event | on-change | logical-tick
                    | frontier-advance | presentation-frame | explicit
authority origin    caller(π) | owner-transition(O) | admitted-evaluator(E)
                    | admitted-provider(P)
materialization     local-only | store | publish-value | publish-relation
                    | adapter-stream | persist
```

The materialization coordinate is a nonempty canonical finite target set over
these kinds: `local-only` is exclusive, value/relation publications are
exclusive, and adapter-stream/persist composition is deferred.

The selected Core carrier is one pure `eval(EP, body)` operation plus typed
request/receipt/result trace records. `OwnerTransition` is the normalized
owner-store form of that operation: its reads, pure calculation, and write are
all owned by O. O validates and serves its FIFO queue, evaluates the RHS at
service, then writes one bounded transition. For
SCN-02 the plan is `state / owner(S) / on-request / caller(self) / store`;
an owner transition does not turn S into the authority origin or grant S's
ambient authority to the requester.

If an evaluation at O needs a T-owned operand where T ≠ O, elaboration either
rejects the unannotated form with `E-EVAL-CROSS-OWNER`, or emits an explicit
`RemoteResult(O→T)` and dependency receipt. The receipt is a visible typed
trace fact and must be created by its request → serve → reply → receive chain
before its use; its target plus the exact caller/producer/target/label release
grant are checked at the producer. It is immutable released information, not
an authority transfer. This decision adds no common snapshot, lock, hidden
requester read, blind stale write, or distributed transaction.

A `DesignatedEval` is a designated form of `eval` that records evaluator
identity, finite policy, trigger, exact canonical finite input-producer set,
version, authority evidence, and observation policy. At a fixed
evaluator/key/frontier it determines one result version and value; a duplicate
publication cannot create another semantic consumption. The designated step
does not consume: an explicit named consumer consumes the already-decided
version once in the bounded M3 profile. A failure records a failure occurrence
and publishes no success value. Consumers may present an already-decided value
but do not semantically re-evaluate it.

## Consequences

- Evaluation site and authority origin are visible in Core and trace rows.
- M3 may infer only the unique default prescribed by the Constitution; an
  equally valid choice yields `E-EVAL-AMBIGUOUS` and requests the smallest
  `EvalPlan` annotation.
- Consumer-local relations and provider-side computation have typed plan
  coordinates now. Relation storage/projection and provider algorithms are
  deferred respectively to M4 and later provider work; neither may mutate
  semantic owner state under its M3 classification.
- `theory/13` gives the shared interface that M4/M5 extend; it does not claim
  their relation, fallback, observation, cut, or patch theorems.

## Non-effects

This decision does not freeze Surface grammar, serialization, public API/ABI,
wire protocol, topology, retry/exactly-once behavior, multi-owner atomicity,
save/load, patching, production identity, deployment, or I1+ completion.
Proof status remains exclusively in theory/11.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider only on a counterexample to owner-only mutation, no hidden
communication/authority, deterministic elaboration, or the absence of a
conservative extension to the M4 relation calculus.
