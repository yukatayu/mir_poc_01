# ADR-0031 — SYS-5 local toy fabric and typed devtools

Decision date: 2026-08-28

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-034's finite headless SYS-5 slice. One ordinary checked source
deterministically projects `WorldAuthority`, `ParticipantA`, `ParticipantB`,
and `ViewerC` artifacts and actual generated endpoints. The accepted workflow
executes owner RMW, designated publication/consume, relation publication and
fallback/reacquire, typed failure, local cut/restore, bounded patch, and finite
verification evidence without runtime source reparse, fixture-name dispatch,
expected-result lookup, manual route, or authority/Core/state injection.

Accept the provisional internal explicit relation-anchor locus and retain it
through AST, M6/M7 Core, projection, and source map. The B-owned relation may
therefore depend on an A-primary anchor, B-fallback anchor, and C-local
projection without equating existence, relation ownership, or observation.
Omitted anchor loci remain legacy bounded inputs and are never inferred; the
SYS-5 leave/fresh path requires an explicit primary locus.

## Authority and lifecycle consequence

A leave request names only the checked relation. M9 derives and retires the
exact source-declared primary membership plus its capability/witness lineage,
then the independent relation owner publishes fallback. A duplicate leave is
typed and failure-closed. Fresh reacquire consumes the exact leave tombstone,
issues a distinct epoch/incarnation and fresh capability/witness lineages, and
only then lets the same relation owner republish primary. Neither receipt,
schedule, locus name, nor transport transfers or creates authority, and the
consumer never mutates the relation.

The selected ST lifecycle transitions are clone-prepared failure-atomic
candidates: capacity, checked anchor, M9, and generated endpoint validation
complete before live commit. Failure preserves live M9/M8/queue/causal/
observer state. A complete post-leave local cut retains the exact retired
lineage and can restore it for fresh reacquire; inconsistent cut evidence
rejects before mutation. These are bounded runtime facts, not hidden
multi-owner transactions, durable persistence, or general theorems.

## Devtools and acceptance consequence

Accept one observer-safe joined report that follows source span → checked Core
→ locus fragments → generated edge → distinct request and runtime occurrences
→ mutation/result/failure/relation/save/patch lifecycle. It marks active,
discarded-post-cut, and restored branches, retains exact patch occurrence and
lifecycle references, and excludes raw credentials, capability/witness
material, private payload/state, and raw M8/M9 identity.

The accepted implementation/evidence cut is
`53a21e64b5a17e24b522f720db10b6e539c058e0`. Fresh evidence is the finite test
matrix recorded by PROPOSAL-034: AST 10/10, M7 27/27, SYS-3 28/28, workflow
8/8, relation 17/17, cut/patch 12/12, CLI 3/3, M9 lifecycle 4/4, full
`mir-runtime` all-targets, preserved M10 2/4/67, format, warnings-denied

`runtime-monitored` only; no Lean, bounded-model, or general theorem status is
added. Independent M9 authority/concurrency, semantics, and usability/security

## Lifecycle consequence

SYS-5 is completed. SYS-6 I2 assurance, conformance, and lifecycle closeout is
the sole active ADR-0026 milestone; SYS-7 is next. Theory remains T1. Broad
PHASE-I1 and official I2 entry/exit remain unaccepted until SYS-6 evaluates
their actual criteria and records fresh whole-profile evidence. Public
contract freeze is not added as an implicit I2 criterion.

## Non-effects and reconsideration

This decision neither freezes final Surface syntax, CLI/API/ABI/wire/JSON or
artifact format; nor accepts browser/View/renderer, sockets, real transport,
multi-process execution, durable save/load, OW1 lifecycle/cut/patch, arbitrary
relation DAG, arbitrary patch/lifecycle commutation, production deployment, or
a general authority/failure-atomicity/reacquire/projection/noninterference
theorem; nor changes accepted M10 behavior.

Reconsider only for an inferred anchor locus, caller-minted lifecycle
authority, M8 mutation before M9 retirement, missing exact leave→fresh lineage
join, partial live mutation after candidate failure, invalid post-leave restore,
invented causal join, observer secret leak, filename/expected-result semantics,
or a SYS-6 direct consumer unable to use the finite evidence conservatively.
