# ADR-0019 — Maintained relation and late projection

## Decision

Under ADR-0015, adopt `theory/14-maintained-relation-projection` as the M4
calculus. A maintained relation is semantic source of truth when it defines a
derived value. Its domain-neutral `RelationDef` and owner-held `BindingState`
are part of the semantic configuration; the consumer is neither its owner nor
its mutation authority.

The owner may publish an admitted **relation** through the M3
`publish-relation` boundary. A consumer evaluates that projection at
`presentation-frame` only with a coherent `PresentationContext`: every needed
anchor sample has the selected relation frontier and expected anchor epoch.
The consumer's calculation is `local-only`. No rule permits a value
publication, absolute-pose stream, adapter stream, hidden communication, or
implicit authority transfer in place of the relation publication.

Semantic fallback is an owner-recorded, lineage-preserving primary-to-fallback
advance caused only by semantic invalidation. It never automatically
re-promotes. Explicit reacquire needs a fresh witness and binding epoch and
begins a new lineage. Presentation gaps, latency, interpolation, prediction,
or LOD remain consumer-local: they return a rendering response but do not
change the semantic relation, binding state, authority, lineage, or history.

The M4 finite label profile is the decidable chain
`public < restricted < private`. A projected release label is the greatest
restriction of its relation label and all presentation input labels; requesting
a weaker label is rejected. Relation cycles, stale anchors, split frames, and
consumer-originated relation mutations are rejected.

## Consequences

- `RelationDef` / `BindingState`, relation activation frontier, and lineage are
  part of the theory/01 configuration and theory/04 provenance closure.
- M4's finite evidence covers projection coherence, monotone fallback/fresh
  reacquire, presentation-gap nonmutation, restriction propagation, and the
  listed rejects. Its exact proof classification is in theory/11.
- SCN-12 is a pressure scenario. Its application vocabulary remains outside
  the domain-neutral Core carrier.

## Non-effects

This decision does not freeze Surface spelling, Core exchange/JSON names,
wire/API/ABI, renderer or transport, general label lattice, arbitrary relation
DAG implementation, save/load algorithm, distributed semantics, conformance,
or product/deployment status. It does not discharge the general fallback or
two-layer-time obligations.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider only on a finite counterexample showing that a coherent relation
projection requires absolute-value materialization, weakens an input label,
permits semantic mutation from a presentation gap, or cannot preserve the
no-auto-repromotion rule.
