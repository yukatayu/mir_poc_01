# ADR-0020 — Shared formal model / metatheory

## Decision

Under ADR-0015, adopt `theory/15-shared-formal-model` as the finite M5 shared
formal source.  The model is one fresh, non-opaque finite universe rather than
an M3/M4 adapter.  It supplies semantic Surface tags/source references,
deterministic `Core | Diagnostic` elaboration, concrete `Config`, `Step`,
`WellFormed`, occurrence/history, observation, relation projection, and a
cut/save/restore interface.

`ResultFrontier`/result version, relation activation frontier, epoch,
presentation context, and atomic cut remain nominally distinct.  The model
uses M3's request/receipt/designated-result carriers and M4's owner-held
relation/binding carrier in the same configuration.  It does not make a
designated-result producer frontier a relation frontier, and it does not make
a consumer presentation frame a configuration, authority, history, or save
carrier.

Owner relation mutation validates the exact owner capability/witness lineage
for the relation, binding epoch, and membership epoch.  Owner
`publish-relation` stores an exact relation/binding projection carrier;
consumer `local-only` evaluation reads that carrier with its separate
presentation context, and every required consumer sample has a separate
admitted release.  The finite checked transform domain rejects overflow, and
the finite label join cannot weaken a relation/input restriction.  SaveObject
retains the finite semantic provenance closure, including a published carrier,
and restore rejects declared stale provenance.

## Consequences

- M6 has one semantics-level source while its concrete grammar remains open.
- M3/M4 foundations remain exact finite regression evidence; their type
  carriers are not imported or silently identified with M5's carrier.
- The finite shared model records the required positive mixed trace and
  consumer-mutation/materialization falsifier.  Proof status is still stated
  only in theory/11 and only for its exact finite scope.
- The model keeps an explicitly inactive patch slot for configuration shape;
  this is not patch semantics or a THM-006 discharge.

## Non-effects

This decision does not finalize Surface syntax, source files/ranges, final
diagnostic identifiers or repairs, public Core/JSON/wire/API/ABI, arbitrary
relation DAGs, general label lattice/noninterference, general fallback,
general cut/save/load, Z-cycle, patch, transport, distributed execution,
runtime conformance, I1 completion, or deployment.  It does not promote a
bounded enumeration or finite theorem into a general theorem.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider only on a finite counterexample showing that the shared carrier
must identify distinct frontiers/cuts/presentation state, cannot represent the
owner-bind → relation-publication → consumer-local projection trace, or admits
consumer semantic materialization/mutation without an explicit owner step.
