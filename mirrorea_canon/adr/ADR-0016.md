# ADR-0016 — Design Constitution and M1 semantic alignment

Decision date: 2026-08-04

## Decision

Adopt `root/design-constitution` under the owner-approved ADR-0015 program.
It is the concise decision filter for cross-cutting Mir v0/I1+ questions; it
does not replace the North Star, theory, specification, scenario suite, or
proof ledger. Later detailed rules must agree with it rather than treating it
as an alternate source of Core fields, grammar, transport, or runtime design.

The following alignment is binding now.

1. In SCN-02, `BrowserClient[self]` is the authority origin and `S` is the
   evaluation locus. Both `player[target].hp` and `player[self].atk` are
   S-owned operands, so S validates the request and evaluates the RMW as one
   bounded owner transition. The requester neither reads S-private values nor
   blind-writes a precomputed value. A genuinely cross-owner operand needs an
   explicit result/receipt path or a v0 Diagnostic; no distributed transaction
   is inferred.
2. Semantic fallback is monotone lineage evolution caused by semantic
   invalidation. Consumer-local sample/packet/rendering loss is presentation
   fallback and does not change semantic lineage, authority, occurrence, or
   fallback position. Re-promotion needs explicit fresh reacquire.
3. ADR-0008 and spec/02 remain a retained pre-M6 Surface profile and historic
   compatibility candidate, not the final v0 grammar. M6 chooses the final
   bounded grammar only after the shared model, comparing that candidate with
   at most one smallest viable alternative. Existing Surface evidence remains
   LAB evidence; it is neither deleted nor promoted by this clarification.

## Consequences

- M2 may bind assertion subjects to the stable C1--C12 Constitution clauses,
  but alone decides assertion artifact/result vocabulary, digest/freshness, and
  lifecycle acceptance records.
- M3 defines exact evaluation/materialization carriers; M4 defines relation
  calculus/projection; M5 updates the shared formal model and proof inventory.
- The SCN-02 expectation is clarified through the ordinary SCN amendment rule;
  its conformance status does not move.

## Non-effects

This decision changes no official T0/G0/T1 state, OBL status, proof result,
conformance result, runtime implementation, public API/ABI/wire format,
transport, production deployment, or permanent non-goal. It adds no Core
constructor, evaluator algorithm, relation schema, serialization, or Surface
syntax production.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider only if the Constitution conflicts with the North Star or a
protected guarantee, or a later milestone shows that a binding rule requires
an owner-reserved decision. Internal carrier and syntax choices remain governed
by the Constitution's decision priority.
