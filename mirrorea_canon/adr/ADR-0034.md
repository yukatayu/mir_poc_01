# ADR-0034 — Owner-delegated Mirrorea I3 Distributed Foundation execution

Decision date: 2026-09-01

## Decision

Adopt the owner disposition in PROPOSAL-037. The active bounded program is
**Mirrorea I3 Distributed Foundation**. It first canonically separates the
semantic strata, project/product layers, and lifecycle phases and fixes future
Browser/Host/package/View/provider responsibility boundaries. It then maps the
accepted I2 generated per-locus artifacts and communication across at least two
OS processes over one selected real reliable-stream transport and closes a
finite C-distributed profile.

The fixed order is ALIGN-0, ALIGN-1, ALIGN-2, I3-0 through I3-6, and NEXT-0.
The sole current LAB roadmap is Plan 250. Only one milestone is active at a
time. The accepted I2 cuts and closed Plan 249 remain regression history; they
are not reopened or used as this program's authority.

## Preserved architecture and trust boundary

Existing semantic `S0--S6`, new project/product `PL-0--PL-6`, and lifecycle
`T0--T2` / `I1--I6` are distinct coordinates. Domain objects remain libraries
or applications. Shared-Space / World-Web receives only a deferred
responsibility boundary, Reversed Library remains separate, and the two
satellites remain separable.

Browser/Host admission, authority grant, View projection, renderer-local
presentation, typed input, typed provider effect, privileged native plugin,
and resource/sandbox responsibility are separate boundaries. View/provider
code may compute presentation-local results but may not own or mutate
authoritative domain semantics. Untrusted Mir packages cannot call raw native
FFI directly. Transport, process, route, session, certificate, package, and
provider identity remain non-authoritative.

## Transport and evidence decision

Retain only TLS-over-TCP framed reliable stream and QUIC reliable stream;
exclude QUIC datagrams. I3-0 may select one only after equal executable
two-process canaries carry the same private source/Core-bound semantic carrier
and exercise the shared authority, fail-closed framing/disconnect,
duplicate/reconnect, no-stale-resurrection, ordering, and observer-safe
diagnostic criteria. A paper-only selection is prohibited.

I3-3 must cover every applicable plan/05 failure-matrix family. I3-4 must emit
the minimum observer-safe evidence required by its C-distributed gates before
I3-5 joins those already-existing facts into a user workflow. No reliable
stream, retry mechanism, or reconnect supplies Mir semantic order, authority,
or exactly-once behavior.

## Lifecycle and assurance consequence

Program activation is not official I3 entry or exit. I3-6 may apply official
I3 entry followed by exit only when its exact source-first multi-process
criteria, finite conformance rows, positive/falsifier evidence, evidence-class
ledger, fresh regression, and independent review all pass. Runtime and
conformance tools remain evidence producers, never lifecycle authority.

Theory remains T1. Broad PHASE-I1 remains unaccepted with its existing
OPEN-026/027 and carrier-freeze criteria. This program may update an obligation
only when its exact new evidence actually changes that obligation; it cannot
move general theory administratively.

## Delegated authority

Within the fixed program, the orchestrator may autonomously change the
authorized Canon, internal architecture, Rust/Lean/model/test/CLI/devtools,
transport comparison and selection, private provisional encoding, process
deployment mapping, multi-process runtime, network faults, finite conformance,
reports, agent configuration, roadmap, and status surfaces. Every milestone
retains a direct consumer, representative falsifier, finite stop condition,
independent review, one report, validation, commit/push, and remote parity.

## Owner-reserved boundary

The program must stop for a North Star or guarantee weakening; concrete domain
Core promotion; unavoidable hidden transaction or retry; irreversible public
contract freeze; both retained transports failing the same criteria; an
irreversible non-migratable semantic tie; production/publication; user-data,
secret, or paid-resource risk; final upper-product/governance/plugin-ABI
decisions; or a reproducible parent-goal/North-Star contradiction.

## Non-effects and reconsideration

This activation does not select transport, implement sockets, enter or exit I3,
change accepted I2 behavior, freeze public grammar/CLI/JSON/API/ABI/wire/
package/FFI, deploy production, select a browser/renderer/sandbox, define
Shared-Space concrete semantics, start Reversed Library product work, complete
I4/I5, or establish general network/security/fairness/durability proofs.

Reconsider only at an owner-reserved stop condition, a reproducible accepted
I2 regression, a current-milestone falsifier, or evidence that the selected
transport cannot conservatively satisfy the fixed failure/order contract. Do
not add a third transport candidate without fresh owner direction.
