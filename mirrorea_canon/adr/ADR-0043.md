# ADR-0043 — I3-3 finite failure/ordering acceptance and owner pause

Decision date: 2026-09-09

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-046 and close I3-3 at source/evidence cut
`fe5dd972e2ddb3a513c785458a07702e4d4d99fa` under ADR-0034's explicit bounded
owner authorization and the subsequent instruction to stop after I3-3.

The accepted profile maps all twenty plan/05 failure families and required
ordering categories to explicit finite positive/falsifier evidence. Actual
two-process QUIC delivery, ambiguity, reconnect, stale/duplicate rejection,
genuine current-authority changes, checked owner ticks, typed read-only provider
crossing and local cut custody are distinguished from LOCAL codec/policy/ledger/
witness/graph checks and the bounded lifecycle model. Transport remains
non-authoritative; source-derived owner evaluation, retained request/history,
typed failure and redaction remain mandatory.

Plan 250 remains the sole authorized current roadmap. Execution is now
owner-paused with no active semantic milestone. I3-4 is next but inactive until
explicit resume; later milestones remain ordered and inactive. This pause is
neither blocked nor stale, does not close the program, and does not enter or
exit the official I3 lifecycle. Historical ADR-0039/0040 pause/resume decisions
are retained rather than rewritten.

## Rationale and rejected expansion

The finite profile gives I3-4 a real generated process/network fault seam with
testable current-authority and ordering behavior. Workspace regression,
retained-causality mutation falsifiers and independent review close its direct
consumer without requiring arbitrary-network theory or durability first.
Expanding the cut into save/restore/live patch, or completing general browser/
provider/reconnect behavior, would open later frontiers without serving this
milestone's remaining falsifier. Those expansions are deferred, not silently
claimed or used to weaken the finite boundary.

## Assurance, consequences and non-effects

Runtime/process evidence is `runtime-monitored`; the exact finite lifecycle
explorer is `model-checked-bounded`, not Lean or a general proof. The verified
source has1573 passing workspace tests, four passing runtime doctests, fresh
format/Clippy gates and final review P0/P1/P2=0. LAB:docs/reports/2606 records the
exact twenty-row/order mapping, commands, cuts and non-claims.

Theory T1, broad PHASE-I1 residuals and theory/11 classifications are unchanged.
SCN C-distributed acceptance, relation/designated pressure, network-devtools
workflow and finite I3 conformance remain I3-4/5/6 work after explicit resume.
No public compatibility, production/WAN, Browser/I5, general cut/reconnect,
distributed durability, upper-domain primitive or exactly-once claim follows.
The admitted local cut produces no saved image and the cancellation case is
header/body-read cancellation plus physical close, not RESET_STREAM or observed
body-write failure.

Reopen only on PROPOSAL-046's reproducible invariant/direct-consumer falsifiers.
