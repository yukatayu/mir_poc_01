# ADR-0041 — Source-declared owner admission budget

Decision date: 2026-09-07

## Decision and authority

Under the owner-delegated bounded authority of ADR-0034, adopt candidate B of
PROPOSAL-044 and spec/16 at integration baseline
`31be54b9ec83b3a75a56f6d71a5523674237b035`. The parent made this bounded
decision after independent contract review; this is not a claim of a new
owner message or accepted implementation.

An opted-in source declares a finite owner-admission tick budget and the
generated typed `DeadlineExpired` failure. Its new initial M6/M7 checked
contract retains source, condition, failure and owner-clock identity through
projection and initial M9 admission. Old sources/failure rows remain unchanged.
The bare M5 owner computation neither absorbs nor proves the new guard.

A serialized owner gate distinguishes pending admission, retained expiry,
current-authority rejection and a one-use serve reservation. Actual downstream
execution, not that reservation, establishes serve/write/success. All checked
execution paths enforce the sealed request-bound permit or reject before
mutation. Typed terminal failure transport/consumption is separate from
successful receipt and uncertain remote disposition. Clock/session/transport
and a stored failure do not grant authority or retry permission.

Candidate A, a separately updated outer lifecycle contract, remains deferred:
the existing M9 layer update does not authorize a request-expiry transition,
and an opaque transport/control bridge cannot fill that semantic gap.

## Scope and acceptance requirements

Spec/16 fixes only the finite source condition, u64 owner-local clock boundary,
ordering, non-bypass, typed outcome and retention contracts needed by I3-3
row 17. Report 2606 remains the sole milestone evidence record. Its direct
implementation gates include static preservation, genuine owner/requester
state transitions, actual two-process QUIC failure delivery, targeted
falsifiers, I2 regression and independent review.

No execution evidence, whole fault-family acceptance or I3-3 close follows
from this decision. Existing M5/M6/M7 Lean evidence is not extended. Theory T1,
broad I1 residuals and official I3 lifecycle remain unchanged. Provider
invocation and general clocks/leases remain separate unresolved consumers;
no public grammar/API/ABI/wire, durability, Browser/Host or domain primitive
is selected.

I3-3 remains the only active milestone. After its complete validation,
independent acceptance review, commit/push and remote parity, honor the latest
owner pause. I3-4 requires explicit resume; Plan 250 remains retained and the
program is not closed by that pause.
