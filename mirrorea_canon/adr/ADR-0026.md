# ADR-0026 — Owner-delegated Mirrorea I2 Systems Foundation execution

Decision date: 2026-08-26

## Decision

Adopt the owner disposition in PROPOSAL-029. The active bounded program is
**Mirrorea I2 Systems Foundation**, with the fixed SYS-0--SYS-7 sequence and
the parent capability goal of deriving and running meaning-preserving
per-locus artifacts and generated communication from one ordinary `.mir`
source inside one operating-system process.

This program is the explicit successor authority required by ADR-0025's I2
entry contract. Within the fixed milestone scope, the orchestrator may apply
evidence-gated Canon, proof/model/runtime, implementation, test, CLI,
governance, roadmap, agent-configuration, report, and status updates without
repeated owner confirmation. ADR-0014 remains the default route outside this
program. ADR-0015 and LAB Plan 247 remain closed records for M0--M10 and grant
no authority to this successor.

The sole current LAB roadmap is
`LAB:plan/249-mirrorea-i2-systems-foundation-current-roadmap.md`. The active
semantic frontier is one SYS milestone. Independent implementation, tests,
formalization, and review may run concurrently only for that same milestone
and semantic candidate.

## Accepted baseline and lifecycle boundary

The immutable implementation/validation baseline remains M10 cut
`23f5a8130334bf0c8516d51e9dcea38b92f50db1`. This program builds on its
accepted finite semantics; it does not make the M10 release facade, hashes,
manifest, or correspondence verifier the architecture of the new runtime.

Activating SYS-0 is not broad PHASE-I1 exit, I2 lifecycle entry, or I2 exit.
The theory lifecycle remains T1. An official lifecycle state changes only when
the actual criteria in `plan/01-phases` have been satisfied by fresh evidence,
independent review, and an explicit authorized acceptance record. An internal
semantic carrier may be accepted without freezing a final public external
contract.

## Milestone and design discipline

Each milestone starts from a capability/contract Goal Statement with a direct
consumer, preserved invariants, representative falsifier, evidence gate, and
finite stop condition. It closes one normative/executable/evidence integration
unit and one report before the next semantic milestone starts. A new research
lane or WRK requires a direct current-milestone consumer and cannot substitute
for closing the milestone report.

For each design question, compare only the smallest current design and one
viable alternative. Decide lexicographically by meaning preservation;
authority/privacy/safety; ordinary Surface; explicit communication/failure/
effect/transaction; small Core; determinism/inspectability; finite
decidability; modular proofability; conservative extensibility;
implementation simplicity; and performance. Stop further generalization when
the direct consumer can use the positive design, its representative falsifier
is detected, the extension boundary is conservative, and independent review
has no major counterexample.

Proof/evidence stays classified as `lean-proved`, `lean-stated`,
`model-checked-bounded`, `runtime-monitored`, or `intentionally-deferred`.
`sorry`, `admit`, theorem-as-`True`, hidden axioms, and bounded enumeration are
not general proof. Release/profile infrastructure remains subordinate to
runtime semantics and reproducibility.

## Preserved architecture and internal carrier authority

The implementation separates semantic runtime kernel, projection/compiler
kernel, dispatch/runtime fabric, conformance evidence generator,
correspondence verifier, release/profile orchestration, and CLI facade.
Communication plans and per-locus artifacts derive only from checked Core and
declared logical topology. Runtime schedule and transport metadata may not
mint Core, authority, capability, witness, semantic state, or expected result.

OPEN-030 may be resolved or narrowly fixed during SYS-1 as a non-public,
typed internal request/reply/receipt contract. It must preserve source/Core
provenance, occurrence order, typed result/failure, visibility/redaction,
membership/incarnation, capability/witness references, and applicable
frontier/consumption state. Receipt does not transfer authority; queue
position is not identity; retry/exactly-once remains explicit and deferred.

## Owner-reserved conditions

The owner retains the stop conditions listed in PROPOSAL-029: North Star or
guarantee weakening; Core promotion of domain vocabulary; hidden multi-owner
transaction; irreversible public API/ABI/wire freeze; real-transport
selection/implementation within this program; production/publication; danger
to user data, secrets, or paid resources; an irreversible observable tie the
Constitution cannot order; or a reproducible parent-goal/North-Star conflict.

## Consequences

- SYS-0 records the baseline, authority, one roadmap, full Goal Statements,
  and meta-alignment matrix, then advances the active goal to SYS-1.
- SYS-1--SYS-6 may change the delegated internal semantic/runtime surfaces
  only when their milestone evidence and independent review support it.
- SYS-7 records an inactive I3 goal and entry contract, then stops without
  implementing real transport.
- Public API/ABI/wire, real transport, durable distributed persistence,
  production, browser renderer, general metatheory, lock-free optimization,
  and I3 implementation remain non-effects.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider only at an owner-reserved stop condition, a reproducible
counterexample to the parent goal, or after SYS-7 closes and a new owner
direction is required. Difficulty, deferred general obligations, lack of
public contract freeze, or incomplete later phases is not itself a reason to
stop this bounded program.
