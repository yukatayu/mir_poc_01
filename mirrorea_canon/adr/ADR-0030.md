# ADR-0030 — SYS-4 in-process generated dispatch runtime

Decision date: 2026-08-27

## Decision

Adopt PROPOSAL-033's crate-private process-local runtime fabric. It consumes an
owned `GlobalProjectionResult`, exact complete sealed M9 admission, and typed
initial values for projected local schemas. It creates locus-tagged runtime
components and endpoints only from the generated communication plan. Runtime
execution never reparses source, selects a fixture plan, writes a manual route,
or accepts caller-supplied Core, authority, semantic state, or expected result.

ST gives each semantic locus its own M8 session. Generated messages cross the
source outbox and target inbox before target-local M9 revalidation and M8
service. Same-owner mutation remains at its owner artifact; relation and
designated paths preserve their checked owner/evaluator/consumer placement.
The accepted four-locus/two-owner pressure case mutates only its two owner-local
stores. No target obtains a direct cross-locus mutable store handle.

## Carrier, retry, failure, and observation consequence

Each actual endpoint record and trace retains the exact checked program,
source/Core, artifact fragments, communication edge, envelope, route,
occurrences, typed success/failure, visibility/redaction, M9 generation, and
publication/consumption lineage applicable to that operation. These facts are
correlation and evidence, not authority.

The designated-result endpoint implements the SYS-3
`ReturnExistingNoNewConsumption` refinement. The first exact named-consumer
delivery invokes M8 semantic consumption once. An exact retry returns the
stored typed decision without another M8 consume. A competing consumer or any
provenance, publication, frontier, policy, visibility, redaction, or cache-
binding mismatch fails closed. This does not change the accepted M10 direct
duplicate-delivery rejection and does not claim network exactly-once.

Route, target, membership, capability, witness, producer/evaluator authority,
duplicate/stale result, receipt/publication, split-frame, payload, provenance,
visibility, redaction, cache, and post-dequeue backend failures are typed. A
rejected carrier is terminally quarantined where applicable and cannot mutate
semantic state, consume a cache entry, mint authority, fabricate an M8 success,
or head-block later accepted work.

For an eligible one-owner artifact, OW1 runs the same generated plans through
its worker-owned M8 session and agrees with ST on the selected observable
result. A noneligible multi-owner artifact rejects precisely. An OW1 observer
snapshot failure is typed and distinct from absence; it never rewrites a
committed semantic result or replays the operation. Observer-safe views omit
raw credential, capability, witness, private payload, and raw M8 identity.

## Cut and patch consequence

Accept a bounded ST whole-fabric cut/restore that covers per-locus M8 state,
stores and trace, endpoint mailboxes and records, pending carriers/faults,
receipts, consumption/cache/publication bindings, counters, causality, M9
authority lifecycle/live floor, admitted-validation counters and observer-safe
audit maps, and patch lifecycle/frontier. Restore validates
the complete cross-record relationship before mutation and can continue an
in-flight accepted delivery exactly once. OW1 cut/restore remains typed
`BackendIneligible`.

Accept a bounded ST quiescent patch that receives only an already checked,
projected, fully M9-admitted designated-expression candidate. Exact base
frontier and authority lineage, topology/schema/owner-route/RMW/relation/non-
designated stability, and shared M9 live-floor rebinding are required. A clone-
prepared candidate is atomically installed only after every check succeeds.
Rejection changes patch lifecycle evidence only. OW1 patch and general patch
compatibility remain `BackendIneligible`/out of scope.

## Non-effects and reconsideration

This decision does not freeze a CLI, public API/ABI/wire/JSON or artifact
format, select deployment or transport, implement sockets/multi-process,
durable persistence, OW1 cut/patch, multi-owner OW, general retry/exactly-once,
arbitrary patch/hot-plug, production relation DAG, browser/View, final
devtools, or a general theorem. It does not alter the accepted M10 profile.

Reconsider only for a reproducible missing/extra generated route, direct
foreign-store mutation, source/fixture reconstruction, authority/provenance/
redaction loss, duplicate semantic consume, selected ST/OW1 mismatch, stale or
forged cut acceptance, rejected-patch mutation, or a SYS-5 direct consumer that
cannot use this typed causal evidence conservatively.
