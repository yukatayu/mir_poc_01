# ADR-0040 — Mirrorea I3 program resume

Decision date: 2026-09-07

## Decision

Adopt the owner disposition in PROPOSAL-043 at source cut
`648425f6bd4304d003d36bc04d346ddf0e78c058`. Resume the original ADR-0034 /
Plan 250 bounded program through NEXT-0. I3-3 is the sole active semantic
milestone; I3-4 through NEXT-0 remain inactive pending the fixed sequence.

This is a resume decision only. It accepts no milestone and changes no
semantic, transport, authority, ordering, failure, or public contract.

## Preserved state and non-effects

The accepted I3-2 profile and owner-pause history in ADR-0039 and
PROPOSAL-042 remain unchanged. The explicit owner instruction satisfies the
pause exception and does not rewrite that history. Theory remains T1, broad
PHASE-I1 remains unaccepted, and official I3 lifecycle entry remains
unentered.

I3-3 is the direct consumer of this decision and may proceed against the
existing Plan 250 / plan/05 criteria. No new network semantics are selected:
the private QUIC reliable stream, deferred TLS-over-TCP comparison baseline,
and excluded QUIC datagrams remain as previously decided. Transport,
session, certificate, process, route, retry, and reconnect facts do not mint
Mir authority or semantic order.

The general pause exception remains available after a later accepted
milestone. Any subsequent milestone still requires its direct consumer,
representative falsifier, finite stop condition, independent review,
validation, and the existing owner-reserved stop rules.
