# PROPOSAL-005 - Delegated L2/L3 theory research

> Historical operating memo. Its owner-maintained exact-target mechanism is
> superseded by PROPOSAL-006 and the amended ADR-0014. It remains evidence of
> the pre-amendment safety analysis.

> Adopted design memo. The effective delegation is ADR-0014 together with
> `plan/02-operating-model` and `meta/agent-instructions`. This memo records
> the owner disposition and does not create a Gate exit, Phase entry, SCN
> result, implementation state, final proof, or public claim.

## Owner Disposition

Recorded on 2026-07-21 and applied through ADR-0014.

- Research agents may autonomously create, compare, formalize, experimentally
  test, provisionally select, supersede, and falsify **reversible L2/L3 working
  theory** in existing LAB lanes. A canon update requires the exact target ID
  and allowed operation in ADR-0014's owner-maintained editable-target table.
- LAB holds candidates, alternatives, countermodels, experiment source, command
  logs, review records, and supersession history. A listed canon target may
  contain only its approved current working proposition; unlisted canon text is
  read-only to this route.
- A steward freezes the rebased integration base, affected blob set, proposed
  diff, evidence digests, and rollback before an independent review. Any change
  to that cut requires a new review. A falsifier freezes reliance immediately
  in LAB, but its canon rollback/demotion also requires independent review.
- The owner retains L0/L1 direction, core primitives, external contracts,
  SCN/Gate/Phase actions, ADR effectivity, final proof/OBL discharge, and
  public completion authority.

## Delegated Envelope

Each research unit must pin the governing canonical and LAB revisions and state
its target IDs, allowed operations, result class, expected falsifier,
non-effects, rollback condition, and integration steward. The steward rebases
and freezes the final canonical base/blob/diff/evidence cut before review. A
later change to that cut invalidates approval until independently re-reviewed.

Allowed result classes are reproduction, literal transcription, countermodel,
conditional lemma, and an experiment inside an existing permitted LAB lane.
Lean artifacts remain LAB evidence. Every `theory/11` status or identity change,
including `stated` and `lean-stated`, requires a separate owner action and is
outside this delegation.

The delegated route never authorizes a semantic change merely because a file is
labelled L2 or L3. It stops for owner action when the work would alter an L0/L1
statement, core or authority primitive, source/wire/provider/transport/public
contract, SCN expectation, conformance classification, Gate/Phase criterion,
final proof status, or moratorium-protected evidence/helper/schema/CI surface.

## Non-effects

This disposition does not select an OBL-020 organization, close Surface v0
grammar, define a new calculus carrier, change theory/11 entries, establish a
proof, create a parser/runtime, alter a frozen scenario, or promote a runnable
LAB artifact to conformance or product status.
