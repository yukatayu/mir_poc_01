## Reopen conditions

A new autonomous L3 successor is eligible only when all of the following are
present: a new source-backed proposition not represented by Plans 225--229 or
WRK-0044; a named independent consumer; a decisive candidate-specific
falsifier; an existing-LAB-lane execution plan requiring no new shared helper,
evidence lane, semantic surface, or reserved dependency; and a reliance claim
strictly below Canon satisfaction, theorem/OBL closure, implementation, and
runtime behavior.

Specific reopen triggers are: a reproducible allegation that WRK-0044's exact
premise bundle is inconsistent or vacuous; a pinned ordinary-design artifact
that consumes finite satisfiability of that exact bundle; a concrete claim that
specified static facts plus specified bridges entail restored one-shot behavior;
or a concrete card with a closed fact/occurrence/order inventory. A changed
Canon cut or reproducible defect may also warrant a fresh screen.
