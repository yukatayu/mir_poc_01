  fiber and relation cell/view fiber;

  restore graph observations;

semantic correlation, occurrence equality, pending semantics, persistence

1. an owner/Canon design decision supplies a concrete semantic premise while
   leaving a separate reversible consistency or countermodel question;
2. relevant Canon wording or the authority cut changes, creating a
   non-duplicate literal audit;

   thereby supplies a real proposition to falsify; or
