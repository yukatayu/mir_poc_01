## Selected pre-registration candidate

`AB-VIEW-REL-PRE` asks:

> On exactly WRK-0037's two opaque request atoms, listed incidental observations,
> frontiers, receipt/resume transitions, failure behavior, dependency ground, and
> local restore, is `DirectView` observationally equivalent to one independently
> stated relation-first presentation whose endpoints remain explicit atoms?

The relation-first side must directly spell phase, checked validation/outcome,
reply, receipt, failure, held disposition, result/provenance, accepted receipt,
resume, dependency ground, mutation, and restore. It may not recover identity
from payload, M1 claims, principal, locus, span, transport/session, queue
context, DAG ancestry, or any other incidental observation.

The registered criterion is two total translations between reachable bundled and
relational states. Their composites must be inverse on reachable states and
preserve every registered observation and transition: acceptance/rejection,
mutation/non-mutation, direct result/provenance ground, and local restore. This
is a finite observational criterion only; it neither asserts definitional
equality nor chooses a future Canon representation.

The exact consumer is the ordinary C2-B/C3 preparation in Plans 208--210. A
pass says only that this finite table does not distinguish the two explicit
presentations. A falsifier retains a distinction for that preparation. Neither
result selects Family A or B.

## Falsifiers, stop lines, and non-effects

The candidate fails for this scope if either translation is partial; either
composite is non-identity on a reachable state; a registered observation or
transition differs; an endpoint is recovered from incidental metadata; a new
identity, authority, freshness, persistence, recovery, or nominal attempt is
needed; or the relation-first side is merely a definitional repackaging.

On a reproducible falsifier, freeze reliance on the resulting WRK evidence,
retain it forward-only, and stop. On definitional repackaging, close as duplicate
and return to `no-candidate`; do not enlarge the table to manufacture a
distinction. Stop and escalate before evidence if work needs a Canon carrier,
equality/reconstruction rule, source or elaboration contract,
Core/Config/history/SaveObject/queue/wire field, OBL/SCN/Gate/Phase movement,
runtime/public behavior, helper, schema, CI/Make target, or a new evidence lane.

This selection has no effect on Canon identity/correlation, authority,
validation, persistence, recovery, source ergonomics, implementation, proof
status, lifecycle, or public completion. It does not make the future ergonomics
criterion a language rule: later inference may be considered only when uniquely
reconstructible with inspectable grounds, never from incidental context.
