## Source question and result

The source question is whether an owner-side positive membership written only
as metanotation,

    owner-terminal-success(q, v : tau)

may have both of these semantic roles:

1. P017 item 2's terminal owner-service success; and
2. P017 item 3's typed owner-result fact.

The answer is **yes, conditionally**. A candidate may explicitly declare that
the one native membership is the typed owner-result fact for the same q and
typed value, with owner-service terminality intrinsic. P012 and P017 require
owner result to remain separate from requester receipt; they do not require
terminal owner success and owner result to be two positive facts, two objects,
or two reusable views.

The membership's payload annotation alone is insufficient. The candidate-local
declaration of the owner-result role is mandatory. This is a fact-role
coincidence, not a declaration of a new result carrier or a conclusion that all
other P017 obligations are satisfied.

## Canon constraints

P012 V1 requires the later design package to state a typed
correlation/result/failure carrier, local binding scope, pending-control and
cut/save/load treatment. P012 R1 requires owner service result and requester
receipt to be separate typed causal steps, with matching receipt before
resumption, correlation, provenance, rejection, redaction, and failure
accounting.

P017 X1 supplies the selected semantic-residence family: an explicit
relation-valued exchange component anchored by q, while leaving arity, field
layout, constructors, and encoding open. Its item 2 requires outstanding and
at-most-one terminal owner success or typed owner-service failure. Item 3
requires typed owner-result and owner-service-failure facts with result and
validation provenance, plus separate requester pending/receipt/use state. Item
4 makes the semantic receipt distinct from owner service.

Those clauses deliberately distinguish owner result from requester receipt. They
do not require a second owner-side fact for terminality versus result. The same
typed membership may therefore carry both roles when the candidate states that
role directly.

## Candidate A and its exact boundary

For each retained presentation, Candidate A is admissible only as this
candidate-local declaration:

    the native owner-terminal-success(q, v : tau) membership
    is the typed owner-result fact for q and v;
    its terminality is intrinsic to that membership.

For A-Sigma, this remains a non-exhaustive view of one primitive whole
q-scoped relation slice. For B-Pi, it remains a native q-anchored membership
without a carrying-family identifier, factor role, shared key, common
record/witness, or result identity.

The selected X1 relation-state family can provide, in a later candidate, the
carrier family contemplated by V1 without a new result object. A does not waive
V1's typed correlation/result/failure-carrier requirement; it merely permits
the result role to reside in the same native membership as owner terminal
success.

A does not establish result provenance, validation provenance, terminal
exclusivity, owner-failure no-mutation, requester receipt/acceptance, one-shot
use, causality, save/load closure, or the exact reply/receipt carrier. P012
OPEN-011 and all such P017 integration rows stay open.

## Disposition and next boundary

The advisory source reading is:

    A is conditionally compatible and is the minimum supported owner-side reading.
    B is permitted as a stronger candidate-local presentation but is not required.
    C is not required as a source-conformance conclusion.

This conclusion does not adopt A, close Plan 233's typed-terminal-success cells,
or authorize a P017 positive model. A later ordinary candidate must separately
declare its H_K, rerun the fact-status screen, and enter the required P017
minimum-model process before a non-OPEN row can be relied on.

The next autonomous research boundary is not another spelling of A. It is to
identify whether P017's still-open result-provenance requirement has a minimal
candidate-native basis that can be compared without selecting a carrier,
identity, causality, persistence, or validation algorithm. If it cannot, the
correct outcome is an explicit stop rather than a hidden provenance field.
