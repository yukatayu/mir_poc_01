## Current disposition and reopen conditions

Do not open `WRK-0015` for this screened family. A later **actual-bridge**
candidate may be selected only when either an existing source gains a second
named relation with a literal mapping, or a Canon action has already fixed the
proof-facing interface needed for literal transcription. It must still have a
distinct adverse branch and its own ADR-0014 pre-registration.

This actual-bridge condition does not narrow ADR-0014. A different bounded L3
candidate in an existing lane may still be selected under its own standing
eligibility check, alternative/falsifier, and non-effects when it is genuinely
non-duplicative. In particular, an eligible reproduction, countermodel,
conditional lemma, or existing-lane experiment does not need to masquerade as
an actual bridge. This revalidation only declines to create such a record from
the diagnostics/authority/time/cut material screened here.

OBL-001 proof-facing decision:

obligation over writes in `c`. This is advice only. It does not identify the
experiment-local `Result` carrier with Canon Core, change an OBL, claim a
proof, select a runtime/API, or move a Gate/Phase. The owner can instead defer
the issue or authorize the already-described artifact-free bridge-design
comparison. `G0-D3`, PROPOSAL-003, PROPOSAL-004, and PROPOSAL-008 remain
