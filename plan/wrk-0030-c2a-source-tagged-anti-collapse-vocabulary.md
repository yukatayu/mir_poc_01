# WRK-0030 C2-A - source-tagged anti-collapse vocabulary

## Role and evidence boundary

This is a **LAB** result artifact for `working/WRK-0030`. It is ordinary
Markdown evidence, pinned to its owning commit after retention. Canon remains
normative. The six headings below are WRK-local questions used to keep cited
source senses separate. They are not Canon objects, carriers, fields,
predicates, relations, occurrence kinds, or a common type vocabulary.

The result is deliberately narrower than a classification: a fact or
comparison placed under one heading does not answer another heading. This
document neither supplies nor denies a relation that Canon has not supplied.

## Retained result

At the WRK-0030 authority cut, the six source-tagged observations can be
retained without a semantic collapse. The only result is **documentary
non-substitution**: placing a source fact or comparison under one
WRK-local question label does not answer a different local question label.

This is independent of WRK-0028's source-role manifest: WRK-0028 records
which current-cut facts and bounded directions exist; this artifact preserves
the non-substitution discipline required when those facts are read across
request, authority, occurrence, and replay wording. It does not repair or
reinterpret frozen WRK-0026.

design, an identity scheme, a replay policy, or proof/implementation evidence.

family, persistence rule, OBL, Gate, Phase, runtime, wire format, API, or

an omitted fact can be inferred only when settled semantics determine it
uniquely and the elaborated artifact retains enough evidence to reconstruct and
check it. This index itself grants no inference or omission.
