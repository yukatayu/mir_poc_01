## Disposition

**No new standing-eligible L3 record is selected at this cut.** No source edit,
Lean/runtime outcome command, helper, schema, validator, CI/Make change, or
generated artifact is created for a successor.

This is not a claim that theory research is complete or that ADR-0014 has been
narrowed. It says only that the current source and consumer dossier does not
justify inventing a successor after two procedure-frozen records.

## Exact reopen conditions

A future triage may select a new record only when one of these concrete inputs
exists:

1. An exact, pinned literal or structural discrepancy in a currently admitted
   LAB lane that is not already covered by WRK-0001 through WRK-0021 or the
   recorded T-RESEARCH audits, plus a named current consumer whose positive and
   adverse outcomes require a specific retain/reject action.
2. An existing second same-carrier relation or literal mapping together with a
   real importer/consumer, or a prior owner/canon disposition of the relevant
   proof-facing interface. A successor may test the resulting bounded fact but
   still may not choose the interface.
3. An owner resolution of the lane-catalog correspondence, or a wholly distinct
   candidate expressible inside currently admitted roots without validator
   widening. This is not permission to widen the catalog autonomously.

Corrected shell quoting, extra Lean instances, altered tactics, changed
relation encodings, or revised commands do not reopen WRK-0020 or WRK-0021.
They would be repairs, not new evidence.
