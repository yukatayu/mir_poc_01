## Current autonomous boundary

Do not create `WRK-0014` merely to make another command run. The current
autonomous action is to maintain evidence integrity and monitor documented
existing lanes for one of these concrete reopen triggers:

1. an exact existing structural mismatch with pinned operands and no invented
   crosswalk, mapping, normalization, or comparison carrier;
2. a documented live downstream decision for which the positive and falsifying
   outcomes select different branches; or
3. an existing bounded lane whose registered question, alternative, falsifier,
   rollback, and non-effects can be stated without touching a reserved surface.

A candidate meeting a trigger still requires its own committed L3
pre-registration before outcome commands run. If the only next move is a
choice of primitive, authority, diagnostic, repair, contract, ledger, Gate,
Phase, conformance, or final proof meaning, prepare an escalation bundle rather
than a WRK.
