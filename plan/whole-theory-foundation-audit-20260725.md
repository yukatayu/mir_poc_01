## Autonomous research screen

No new L3 package is selected from the issues examined here. The apparent
experiments fail at least one current selection boundary:

1. Read-value, service/admission, and request-context experiments would select
   the exact Core, occurrence, or authority relation already reserved by
   PROPOSAL-012/013.
2. Totality and cost experiments repeat retained countermodels and owner
   packets PROPOSAL-008/011.
3. Observation-origin work would repair frozen WRK-0018 or choose a new
   provenance relation without a currently selected consumer.
4. Cut/checkpoint, load, stream, and tombstone experiments require a state,
   restoration, carrier, or theorem interface that Canon has not selected.
5. The T0 profile conflict is an exact Canon contract issue, while Gate-status
   wording is a Canon reconciliation issue, not delegated research.

This is not a claim that theory is complete or that ADR-0014 has become more
restrictive. Current standing eligibility remains governed by ADR-0014 and
the controlling operating route. A new L3 candidate may be screened again
when a real non-reserved discrepancy, an already selected relation with a real
importer, or a non-defer owner/canon action appears.
