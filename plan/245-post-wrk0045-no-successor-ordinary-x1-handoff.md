## Exact current result

WRK-0045 front matter remains `status: L3-open`, while its **Reliance status**
is `frozen` and its result is `DEFER`. The distinction matters: the frozen
marker stops downstream reliance; it is not a new Canon lifecycle status and
does not promote or otherwise rewrite the preregistration.

The retained source compiled structurally under `lean --trust=0`, but its exact
countermodel contains one requester, one binding, and two distinct `Bool`
branches with `pending` true for both. The source's binding non-sharing premise
forces the requester to agree, not the branch. This reproduces the
pre-registered branch-to-binding non-sharing falsifier.

This is a candidate-local failure. P017 requires each in-scope request
occurrence to have one pending administrative binding and prohibits effective
state sharing across distinct request occurrences. It does not define the
candidate's `Branch` carrier or state that two branches of one request must
have distinct bindings. No branch-to-request bridge may be inferred here.

Report 2564's phrase that its adversarial harnesses "cover" pending is an
inventory of harness families, not a proof that every candidate-local pending
law held. The later exact countermodel is the controlling evidence for the
missing branch-to-binding law. The immutable report and frozen record are not
rewritten.

## Reopen conditions

Another autonomous L3 record is eligible only when every condition holds:

1. a materially new, pinned source-backed proposition exists; it is not a
   renamed predicate, a repair of WRK-0045, a restatement of P017, or another
   test of the recorded branch-sharing defect;
2. a named consumer has a substantive conclusion that is not definitionally or
   syntactically the proposed premise;
3. a new candidate-specific falsifier satisfies the unrelated premises while
   violating that conclusion;
4. the experiment fits an existing LAB lane without a new helper, schema,
   CI/Make surface, production artifact, or public interface;
5. it requires no branch/receipt key, request identity, relation schema,
   transition, occurrence rule, causal generator, failure row, history field,
   persistence mechanism, source form, runtime, transport, or public contract;
   and
6. it is a forward-only ADR-0014 preregistration with a fresh authority cut,
   alternative, falsifier, rollback rule, non-effects, and reliance ceiling.

A changed Canon cut or a pinned ordinary-design artifact with a concrete
fact/occurrence/order inventory may satisfy the first condition later. The
current countermodel alone does not.

## Ordinary X1 handoff

The next self-driven package may prepare, but not select, the minimum
candidate-native inventory an ordinary P017 X1 design review would need:

1. the request-occurrence scope and the exact distinction between per-request
   pending association and candidate-local branch facts;
2. the owner branch/type/provenance facts and their failure-row boundary;
3. the requester receipt/matching/rejection boundary;
4. the accepted-use and `Gamma`/`Delta` disposition boundary; and
5. the occurrence/order and save/load closure inventory for every fact actually
   relied on.

This is the existing R/B/T/U/C/L design vector from Plan 227. It may identify
which unresolved coordinate blocks a future proposition, but it must not fill
the coordinate with an implementation convenience or call a future design
choice settled Canon semantics. A later owner-facing proposal, not this plan,
may select an explicit relation-state presentation or request a Canon
amendment.
