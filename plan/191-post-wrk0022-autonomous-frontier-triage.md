## Disposition

## Exact reopen conditions

Re-screen only when at least one of the following exists in the then-current
tree:

1. an exact, pinned discrepancy in an admitted LAB lane with a named current
   binary retain/reject consumer, non-duplicate falsifier, and non-reserved
   adverse branch;
2. an existing second same-carrier relation or literal mapping with a real
   importer/consumer; or
3. an owner/canon disposition that removes the exact boundary needed by a
   candidate, followed by a fresh eligibility screen.

The change must not merely alter a command, import path, tactic, relation
encoding, or helper around a frozen record.
