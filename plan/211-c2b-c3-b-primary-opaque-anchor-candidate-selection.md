## Selected pre-registration candidate

`B2-OPAQUE-PRE` asks whether **one fixed, artifact-local finite model** can
realize every required staged projection without a second nominal attempt
identity. Its two request atoms `q0` and `q1` are distinct by local
construction, yet share all incidental observations: payload, M1 claims,
principal, source and owner loci, source span, declared failure row,
transport/session observation, queue context, and relevant predecessor shape.
No equality, correlation, or authority is inferred from those observations.

The candidate's model-local vocabulary must explicitly account for:

```text
phase(q)     awaiting | replied | received | consumed | failed
outcome(q)   authoritative facts checked plus success or failure
reply(q)     reply role, visible value, provenance, redaction
receipt(q)   reply role and requester receipt role
failure(q)   owner failure role and reason
held(q)      Gamma, Delta, and exact branch disposition
resume(q)    accepted receipt, consumed result/provenance, computed ground
dep(q)       later occurrence and direct consumed-result ground
restore      an injective saved-request to loaded-request reconstruction
```

This is a signature inside one finite experiment. It must not state that any
entry exists in Canon `Config`, `H`, `SaveObject`, a queue, an IR, or a wire
envelope. `restore` is explicit and may rename atoms; it is not implicit token
equality or a claim across independent loads. The one-shot claim is one
accepted receipt and one pure resume in one admissible restored-prefix
extension, never transport exactly-once.

The candidate must check equivariance under swapping `q0` and `q1`: the swap
may exchange their keyed projections but may neither merge them nor select one
through an incidental observation. A later dependent occurrence must carry an
explicit consumed-result ground; program order or request ancestry is not a
substitute.

## Preregistered falsifiers and stop lines

The candidate fails for this scope if any one condition occurs:

1. equal incidental observations alias `q0` and `q1`;
2. reply, receipt, failure, or dependency is recovered only from DAG reachability,
   payload, span, locus, transport, or queue position;
3. duplicate, late, or wrong-locus receipt causes another accepted receipt or resume;
4. a failed request has a matching success continuation, dependent occurrence, or mutation;
5. any awaiting/replied/received/consumed/failed frontier loses phase, provenance,
   held-context disposition, or direct dependency after the stated restore;
6. the model needs a second nominal attempt/exchange identity, in which case Family C
   becomes a concrete comparison obligation; or
7. q, its M1 claims, receipt, locus, or provenance is used as authority.

Stop and escalate rather than repairing the candidate if it requires a Canon
Core/judgment/occurrence primitive, a selected equality rule, a Config/history/
SaveObject/queue/wire field, an OPEN-010/011 resolution, source syntax or
omission, a helper/schema/CI surface, a THM/OBL statement, Gate/Phase movement,
or runtime/public behavior.
