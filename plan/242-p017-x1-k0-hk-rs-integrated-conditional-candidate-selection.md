# Plan 242: P017 X1 K0 H_K-rs Integrated Conditional Candidate Selection

## Role and authority

This LAB selection follows Plan 241. `mirrorea_canon/` remains normative.
The result selects the sole active presentation for a *possible future*
ADR-0014 L3 preregistration; it is not that preregistration and opens no
`working/WRK-*` record. It creates no Canon decision, relation schema, identity
or equality rule, occurrence kind, Core form, step rule, causal generator,
failure row, Config/SaveObject placement, source form, runtime behavior,
theorem/OBL status, Gate/Phase movement, implementation, or public claim.

The selected research question is deliberately conditional. A candidate history
may contain `q`, `s`, and `r` only as explicitly enumerated hypotheses. It may
ask whether those hypotheses have a coherent bounded account; it may not claim
that current MirCore generates, schedules, delivers, enables, or reaches `r`.

## Evidence cut and independent delta

DIRECT Canon constraints are ADR-0014, P012 V1/R1, P013 M1, P017 X1, and
theory/01, theory/02, theory/04, theory/05, and theory/07. Plans 227--241 and
WRK-0044 are LAB evidence. A temporary Oracle review independently challenged
the selection. Its conclusion is advisory only; this plan follows the cited
repository sources.

WRK-0044 retained five static pre-load/restored witness pairs under explicit
premises. It did not declare a causal relation or generator instance, a
candidate requester receipt occurrence, semantic receipt matching, acceptance
or use, or an `r`-sensitive cut. Its causal row was expressly a textual
conditional mapping and established no semantic transition or reachability.

`H_K-rs` therefore has a new source-to-consumer delta, while adding no new P017
review category:

| New candidate-local premise | Consumer absent from WRK-0044 |
| --- | --- |
| direct `send -> receive` roles over `(q, s)` | request-to-service generator accounting |
| co-located reply-send role on `s` | reply projection without a second occurrence |
| distinct requester-locus candidate occurrence `r` | a possible semantic-receipt endpoint |
| direct `send -> receive` roles over `(s, r)` | result-to-receipt causal accounting |
| exact typed result-to-receipt relation and functional matching | non-sharing / at-most-one receipt association |
| cut after `s` and before `r`, plus a cut containing `r` | channel closure and restore review for the receipt endpoint |

This is independent only when the later source makes those consumers
load-bearing. A source that merely derives `q prec r`, proves acyclicity, or
repeats WRK-0044's static frontier table is a duplicate and must not be
registered.

## Selection

| Alternative | Disposition | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A-Sigma integrated q-scoped relation fiber | **selected for one possible L3 experiment** | One primitive candidate-local semantic relation residence isolates the new occurrence/role/matching/restore delta without adding factor plurality or coherence machinery. |
| B-Pi factorized relation families | not selected | Its independently primitive families, coherence condition, and joint correspondence add a separate primitive-placement question. They would confound this test and may become a hidden witness or identity. |
| DEFER | **mandatory fail-closed result** | Select it whenever a required account is still open, the delta is duplicate, a stop condition occurs, or candidate hypotheses cannot be made explicit and non-vacuous. |

`A-Sigma` is a candidate-local presentation, not a Canon preference. Its
q-scoped fiber is an extensional relation restriction only: not an exchange
object, field record, identifier, equality subject, saved key, transport
correlation, public token, or cross-load anchor. Its whole-slice
correspondence is not equality, a restore function, or a storage format. If
fact-level matching or restore closure cannot be reviewed without turning the
fiber into one of those surfaces, the result is `DEFER`; this record must not
switch to B-Pi after observing that failure.

## Required future preregistration

A later `WRK-0045` may be created only after this selection is committed and
pushed, a fresh exact Canon/LAB source cut is pinned, and the ADR-0014 standing
predicate is rechecked at that cut. It must use the existing `plan/`
Markdown-held Lean lane and introduce no helper, module, schema, CI/Make
surface, or new evidence lane.

The preregistration must include all of the following before any source or
outcome command exists:

1. The status quo: Canon fixes `q`, successful `s`, and `q prec s`, but not a
   successful requester receipt endpoint, receipt carrier, or direct generator
   assignment. `q prec s` is not evidence for `H_sr1`.
2. One active A-Sigma hypothesis ledger: q-scoped residence and abstract
   whole-slice correspondence; exactly-one non-shared pending binding;
   positive M1/consulted-provenance facts that remain non-authoritative;
   positive owner outstanding and exclusive typed success/failure bases; an
   exact typed result; `H_sr1`, `H_sproj`, `H_r`, `H_sr2`,
   `H_result-send`, `H_receipt`, `H_match`, acceptance/use, authority-order,
   K0, restore, and non-vacuity hypotheses.
3. The declared `D_K` ceiling: role aliases, transitive `q prec r`, strict
   distinctness, and receipt/accepted/consumed views only after their positive
   premises or an actual uniqueness argument. No definition may add a tag,
   tuple, record, common witness, fixed role index, key, choice function,
   closed-world fact, or cross-load equality.
4. A separate success, owner-failure, raw-rejection, accepted/unconsumed,
   consumed, post-service/pre-receipt cut, post-receipt cut, and restore
   obligation or countermodel. Results may not rely on empty domains,
   contradiction, an aggregate desired-result premise, or an uninhabited
   frontier.
5. An explicit alternative: literal C endpoint if one exists at the pinned
   cut, otherwise unavailable; and `DEFER` as the only active alternative.
   B-Pi is recorded as not selected, not as an in-record fallback.

The future result ceiling is one conditional lemma, countermodel, or
existing-lane experiment. It may state that an extensional candidate history
models the declared premises; it may not state P017 satisfaction, MirCore
satisfiability, operational trace existence, or public implementation
readiness.

## Falsifiers and reserved-boundary stops

| Screen | Decisive negative outcome |
| --- | --- |
| independence | retained source does not materially use `H_r`, `H_sr2`, `H_result-send`, `H_receipt`, `H_match`, or r-sensitive closure |
| identity / matching | matching requires a fiber identity, result/reply/exchange ID, saved key, common witness, fixed tuple, span, payload, adjacency, queue position, locus, principal, transport data, or store equality |
| typing / authority | receive role is treated as receipt typing; raw payload chooses the result; M1, provenance, relation membership, anchor, locus, transport, or edge acts as authority |
| failure / K0 | raw rejection creates semantic state or an occurrence; requester rejection is folded into owner failure; failed owner branch gains `s/r/accept/use` |
| causality | `q prec s` or `s prec r` is used as a direct-generator proof; relation/coherence/acceptance/restore generates an edge; reply projection creates a second event; consumption is added to history solely to make an edge |
| restore | correspondence hides loss, merge, duplication, reset, revalidation, stale-authority resurrection, or reconstruction from incidental data; a cut containing `r` lacks predecessors or the middle cut lacks channel closure |
| observability / vacuity | relation, history, or save residence is treated as observable; a result depends on an empty, contradictory, or uninhabited case |
| reserved surface | any Core/type/occurrence/history/schema/failure/Config/SaveObject/source/runtime/API/proof-status surface is needed |

Every listed outcome selects `DEFER` or an ordinary Canon escalation. It never
permits repair by adding a receiver constructor, `[E-RECEIVE]` rule, receipt
type, result carrier, identity, persistence scheme, failure member, causal
generator, observer projection, or runtime behavior.
