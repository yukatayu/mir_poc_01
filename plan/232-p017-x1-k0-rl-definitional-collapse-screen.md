## Directional interpretation screen

### B-Pi to A-Sigma

Common constraints translate, but B-Pi's primitive plurality does not legally
produce one A-Sigma semantic fiber. A tuple/record of family slices adds a
fixed inventory; a tagged union adds tags and a common carrier; an existential
coherent package adds a common witness and, if non-unique, choice or
uniqueness; a higher-arity conjunction adds a joint tuple relation. Treating
coherence as the fiber loses its status as compatibility and fails to contain
the independently primitive family facts.

Minimal separating presentation: one `q` has two disjoint primitive family
facts `P(q, p)` and `O(q, o)`, static compatibility, and joint abstract
correspondence, but no common carrier, tag, pairing relation, record, or
object. This satisfies current B-Pi. One A-Sigma relation containing both
facts needs an excluded aggregation surface; taking only one family loses the
other residence.

### A-Sigma to B-Pi

Common constraints translate, but A-Sigma's primitive unity does not legally
produce independently primitive B-Pi residences. Predicates or projections
over the fiber require a selected factorization and fixed roles, and are
derived views rather than primitive families. Aliasing the fiber gives
duplicate names, not factorization. A partition needs a tag or predicate;
using the whole fiber as coherence makes it a hidden common witness.

Minimal separating presentation: one `q` has one opaque primitive relation
extension containing a pending association and one whole-slice abstract
correspondence, with no role predicates, tags, or named subrelations. This
satisfies current A-Sigma. Producing two B-Pi residences requires a split,
duplication, emptiness, or latent common fiber; none supplies B-Pi's native
primitive plurality within the screen rule.

| Direction | Common constraints | Native delta | Required excluded addition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B-Pi to A-Sigma | erasably translatable | not erasably translatable | aggregate carrier, fixed inventory, common package witness, or joint tuple relation |
| A-Sigma to B-Pi | erasably translatable | not erasably translatable | factorization predicates, family roles, static coherence, or common fiber witness |

The exact-one pending condition does not repair either direction. Reusing it
as a packaging or coherence witness turns an administrative binding into an
unselected key or identity.

## Disposition, elimination, and reopen

Retain one coupled **OPEN** R/L delta, not two complete candidate models. The
current separation is directly stateable: primitive unity cannot be recovered
from primitive plurality without aggregation, and primitive plurality cannot
be recovered from primitive unity without factorization and coherence. This
does not select A-Sigma or B-Pi, prove P017 satisfaction, or establish that
either presentation will survive a completed candidate card.

The fork must collapse later if a completed card supplies legal definitions in
both directions without tags, fixed family index, tuples, records, common
witnesses, new existence/uniqueness/choice/equality, additional coherence,
additional restore assumptions, or a fixed projection/field list. Eliminate a
presentation if expressing its own native delta needs any of those surfaces.

Stop for ordinary Canon process when the card needs concrete schema/field
layout, relation role enumeration, key/public/global identity,
transition/lifecycle, failure member/dynamic row, validation/mutation
algorithm, occurrence kind/causal generator, Config/SaveObject/channel field,
restore function, serialization, source/runtime/API surface, theorem/OBL,
conformance, Gate, Phase, or public claim.
